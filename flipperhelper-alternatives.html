Quick decision tree

  • Need Android or web? → Flippd (multi-platform tracker)
  • Need cross-listing across eBay / Vinted / Poshmark / Facebook? → Vendoo or List Perfectly (different category — see tracker vs cross-lister)
  • Want zero cost and don’t mind manual setup? → Google Sheets with the free FlipperHelper template
  • Need built-in barcode scanning and eBay sold-price lookup? → Flippd
  • Have fewer than 30 active items? → Paper notebook (genuinely fine at that scale)
  • Use iPhone and source at car boots / charity shops with no signal? → FlipperHelper is built for that case

Alternative

Vendoo (different category)

Best for: resellers who list on 3+ platforms and spend hours copying listings.

Cross-listing
Create a listing once, push to eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, etc.
Auto-delist
Auto-delists from other platforms when item sells
Free tier
3 new listings per month
Paid plans
$8.99/month (25 listings) to $69.99/month (unlimited)

Important: Vendoo is not a profit tracker. It doesn’t track expenses, mileage, entry fees, or real profit. It solves “how do I list faster?”, not “am I making money?”. Many resellers use one of each.

Alternative

Google Sheets (free, manual)

Best for: resellers who want zero cost, full control of the data format, and don’t mind manual entry.

Cost
Free, accessible from any device with a browser
Flexibility
Customisable column layout, formulas, charts
Backup
Easy to back up, export, and share with an accountant
Template
Can use the free FlipperHelper template as a starting point

Trade-off vs FlipperHelper: no offline-first mobile entry at the car boot, no photos attached to rows, no automatic profit calculations after fees, and you have to remember to update three places (purchases / listings / sales) when one item moves through the pipeline.

Alternative

Pen and paper

Best for: resellers with fewer than ~30 active items, or anyone testing whether reselling is worth pursuing before committing to a system.

If you only have 5–30 items at any one time, a paper notebook works fine. Where it falls apart: end-of-year tax preparation, multi-platform listing tracking, and remembering which items came from which sourcing trip.

More options

Other options worth knowing about

  • Notion or Airtable — flexible databases, good for multi-stage pipelines, but no native mobile-first reselling workflow.
  • List Perfectly — established cross-lister, similar problem space to Vendoo.
  • Resellr / ResellTrackr / Profit Prophet — smaller-niche profit trackers; feature sets vary.
Common questions

FAQ

What are the best alternatives to FlipperHelper?

It depends on what problem you're solving. If you need a web app, FlipperHelper is mobile only (iOS and Android) — Flippd also works on web. If you need cross-listing (one listing pushed to many marketplaces), Vendoo and List Perfectly are the established tools. If you want a free option and don't mind manual setup, a Google Sheets template can work indefinitely. If you have fewer than 30 active items, even pen and paper is viable.

Is Flippd or FlipperHelper better?

Flippd is multi-platform (iOS, Android, web) and has built-in price research (barcode scanning, image-based eBay sold-price lookups). It's $9.99/month with a free tier capped at 20 items lifetime. FlipperHelper is on iOS and Android and has no built-in price research, but it's free, offline-first, has Hauls (per-trip profit grouping), and tracks 16 selling platforms. Choose Flippd if you need price research or a web app; choose FlipperHelper if you source at car boots without signal and want zero subscription cost.

Is Vendoo a FlipperHelper alternative?

Not really — they solve different problems. Vendoo is a cross-lister: it pushes one listing to multiple marketplaces and auto-delists when an item sells. FlipperHelper is a profit tracker: it logs purchases, expenses, and real profit per item. Many serious resellers eventually use both — a tracker for the money side and a cross-lister for the listing side. See the FlipperHelper vs Flippd vs Vendoo comparison for details.

Can I use Google Sheets instead of a reseller app?

Yes — many resellers track their inventory in Google Sheets or Excel for years. The advantages: free, fully under your control, easy to back up and customise. The disadvantages: no offline mobile entry at car boots, no photos attached to rows, no automatic profit calculations after fees, and you have to remember to update three places when a single sale happens. There's a free reselling spreadsheet template on the FlipperHelper blog that you can use as a starting point.
