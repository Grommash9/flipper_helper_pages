FlipperHelper Pricing

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Pricing

The short version

FlipperHelper is free. No subscription. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account required. As of August 2026, every feature is available at zero cost.

Included

What “free” includes

  • Unlimited items, listings, sales, hauls, and expenses
  • 16 selling platforms tracked (eBay, Vinted, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, etc.)
  • Real profit calculation after every cost type (entry fees, transport, packaging, platform fees)
  • Multi-currency support
  • Offline-first — everything works at car boots with no signal
  • Automatic backup — iCloud on iPhone, folder backup on Android
  • Optional Google Drive image export
  • ZIP export and CSV-to-Google-Sheets export
  • Top Flips and shareable visual cards
  • Money Flow dashboard (revenue, costs, profit by week / month / custom range)
  • Search across entire inventory
Roadmap

What’s on the paid roadmap

The first paid feature will be eBay API integration — automatic detection of when something sells on eBay so you don’t have to mark it sold manually, with fees broken down per item the moment it lands. It also marks items as listed and adds their title if you have left it empty. eBay comes first, with other platforms to follow. This is genuinely new functionality that costs the developer money to provide (eBay API access has commercial terms), so it makes sense as a paid tier.

A web portal is also on the roadmap — your whole business on a big screen, in the browser.

One account (sign in with Apple or Google) and offline-capable sync across phone, tablet, and web are planned too, so every device stays in step even without a signal.

Together these make up FlipperHelper Pro. Pricing hasn’t been finalised. When it launches, this page will be updated with the exact number — join the Pro waitlist on the homepage to hear first.

Commitments

What will NOT happen

  • Existing free features will not be moved behind a paywall
  • No ads will be added to the free experience
  • No required account creation
  • No data harvesting or selling — FlipperHelper has no aggregated user dataset and the developer can’t sell what he doesn’t have
  • No surprise auto-renewal trials
Verify

How to verify pricing

The Apple App Store listing is the source of truth. If you ever see anything other than “Free” on the App Store page, that’s when something has changed. This page will be updated to match.

Common questions

Quick answers

How much does FlipperHelper cost?

Zero. FlipperHelper is free to download and free to use as of August 2026. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. No account is required.

Will FlipperHelper stay free?

Existing free features will stay free. FlipperHelper Pro is in development — a subscription bundling a web portal, eBay API integration for automatic sale syncing, one account across devices, and offline-capable multi-device sync. These are new capabilities, not existing ones being moved behind a paywall. Inventory tracking, expense tracking, hauls, profit calculation, multi-currency, 16-platform tracking, Google Drive export, and ZIP export will remain free regardless.

Are there any hidden fees or in-app purchases?

No. There are no hidden fees, no in-app purchases, no trial periods that auto-convert, and no premium tiers as of August 2026. The App Store listing is the source of truth for pricing — if you ever see a price there, that's the only place a charge would come from.

Why is FlipperHelper free if other reseller apps charge?

FlipperHelper is built by a solo developer for his wife's reselling business, then opened up to other resellers. The app has near-zero marginal cost to run because it's offline-first — there's no backend server farm to pay for. Free is sustainable until FlipperHelper Pro ships, at which point the web portal, eBay sync, and cross-device access are what get paid.

Does FlipperHelper require a credit card or account?

No. There is no account creation step, no email signup, no credit card requirement. Open the app and start logging items immediately. Optional account-based features (Google Drive export) only kick in if you choose to enable them.
