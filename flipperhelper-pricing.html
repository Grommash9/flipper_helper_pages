FlipperHelper Pricing

Last updated: 31 August 2026

No catch

The short version

FlipperHelper is free. No subscription. No ads. No in-app purchases. No account required. Every feature in the app today is available at zero cost.

What’s included

What “free” includes

  • Unlimited items, listings, sales, hauls, and expenses
  • 16 selling platforms tracked (eBay, Vinted, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, etc.)
  • Real profit calculation after every cost type (entry fees, transport, packaging, platform fees)
  • Multi-currency support
  • Offline-first — everything works at car boots with no signal
  • Automatic backup — iCloud on iPhone, folder backup on Android
  • Optional Google Drive image export
  • ZIP export and CSV-to-Google-Sheets export
  • Top Flips and shareable visual cards
  • Money Flow dashboard (revenue, costs, profit by week / month / custom range)
  • Search across entire inventory
What’s next

FlipperHelper Pro

Same tracker, less work

In development

Pro is a subscription for when the business grows: your records on every screen, and sales that record themselves.

Web portal
Your whole business on a big screen. Items, hauls, expenses, real profit — in the browser. 20 finds after a Sunday boot? Type them on a proper keyboard, eBay open in the next tab.
eBay connect
Listings and sales record themselves. Link your eBay account — list an item and it's marked listed automatically, then sold orders land in the app with fees broken down per item. Real profit after every fee, no spreadsheet gymnastics. eBay first, other platforms to follow.
One account
Sign in with Apple or Google. One account keeps your phone and the web portal on the same page. Set up in seconds.
Every device
Phone, tablet and web — at the same time, even offline. Log finds on any device with no signal; everything syncs up the moment you're back online. No conflicts, no double entry.

Everything that's free today stays free. Pro only adds on top.

Pricing for Pro hasn’t been announced. When it launches, this page will be updated with the details — join the Pro waitlist on the homepage to hear first.

Our commitment

What will NOT happen

  • Existing free features will not be moved behind a paywall
  • No ads will be added to the free experience
  • No required account creation
  • No data harvesting or selling — FlipperHelper has no aggregated user dataset and the developer can’t sell what he doesn’t have
  • No surprise auto-renewal trials
Source of truth

How to verify pricing

The Apple App Store listing is the source of truth. If you ever see anything other than “Free” on the App Store page, that’s when something has changed. This page will be updated to match.

Common questions

Quick answers

How much does FlipperHelper cost?

Zero. FlipperHelper is free to download and free to use as of May 2026. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. No account is required.

Will FlipperHelper stay free?

Existing free features will stay free. FlipperHelper Pro is in development — a subscription bundling a web portal, eBay connect (listings and sales record themselves), one account across devices, and offline-capable multi-device sync. Inventory tracking, expense tracking, hauls, profit calculation, multi-currency, 16-platform tracking, Google Drive export, and ZIP export will remain free regardless.

Are there any hidden fees or in-app purchases?

No. There are no hidden fees, no in-app purchases, no trial periods that auto-convert, and no premium tiers as of May 2026. The App Store listing is the source of truth for pricing — if you ever see a price there, that's the only place a charge would come from.

Why is FlipperHelper free if other reseller apps charge?

FlipperHelper is built by a solo developer for his wife's reselling business, then opened up to other resellers. The app has near-zero marginal cost to run because it's offline-first — there's no backend server farm to pay for. Free is sustainable until FlipperHelper Pro ships, at which point the web portal and cross-device sync are what get paid.

Does FlipperHelper require a credit card or account?

No. There is no account creation step, no email signup, no credit card requirement. Open the app and start logging items immediately. Optional account-based features (Google Drive export) only kick in if you choose to enable them.
