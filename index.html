A day in the life — sorted

You know this Sunday. So does the app.

FlipperHelper was built around one real sourcing day, repeated hundreds of times. Follow it through, and you'll see exactly where it earns its place in your pocket.

05:50 · The boot opens

Muddy field. No signal. Three finds in ten minutes.

This is the moment records usually don't happen. Hands full, queue moving, "I'll log it when I get home" — and by evening the prices have blurred together.

FlipperHelper works fully offline. Snap a photo, type the price, done — the app fills in the market by GPS and gives the item its own SKU. Ten seconds per find, at the moment of purchase, not from memory.

11:30 · Home with the haul

£3.50 entry, £12 of fuel, a bacon roll. Who's paying for that?

Trip costs are where spreadsheet profit quietly turns into fiction — they never make it into the per-item maths.

Log the day's expenses once, and the app splits them across everything you bought. Entry fees, transport — car, train, even the van rental for the France run. Each find carries its true cost from day one, in any of 6 currencies with daily rates. Your numbers, your call.

Tuesday evening · Listing night

"Wait — is the teapot on eBay or Vinted?"

Fifty live listings across four platforms is where memory gives up. Items sit forgotten in drawers while their listings quietly expire.

Mark where each item is listed — across 16 platforms — with dates and asking prices. Platform badges show the state of every find at a glance, and a reminder nudges you about the ones still waiting in the drawer. Your call when.

Day 9 · It sells

Sold for £28. But what did you make?

Turnover is what resellers brag about. Profit — after the platform's cut, the postage, the trip that found it — is what pays for anything.

Record the sale and the app closes the item's path: purchase, trip share, fees — one honest number, in green. Real profit, correctly counted. And when it's a good one, a share card is ready for your socials.

Month end · The honest look

Which markets are worth the 5am alarm?

Without the numbers it's all gut feel — you keep going back to markets that stopped paying months ago, and tax season arrives as a shoebox of guesses.

The Money Flow dashboard shows what you made, what it cost, and how much cash is sitting in unsold stock — by week, month or any range. Per-market and per-trip totals tell you which Sundays earn their alarm. And one tap exports the lot to Google Sheets — a tidy, dated record when Self Assessment comes calling.

Any day · New phone

Months of records. One device. Sleep well anyway.

The quiet fear of every tracker: drop the phone in a puddle at the boot, and a year of your business goes with it.

Backups run automatically — iCloud on iPhone, a folder you choose on Android — and everything exports as one file that works on both platforms. Switching phones, or even switching sides between iPhone and Android, is a five-minute non-event.

Why this exists

Built at the car boot, not in a boardroom

FlipperHelper started because my wife Valentina needed a better way to run her reselling business. Her spreadsheet kept getting in the way: a buy logged at the stall, then a forgotten listing, a missed fee, per-item maths gone wrong.

So I built her an app. She's been the lead tester ever since — every Sunday, boot after boot.

"If a feature doesn't survive a Sunday morning at a car boot, it doesn't ship."

We source around England, drive to France for vintage, and sell on eBay, Vinted and Facebook Marketplace. Everything on this site — the app, the guides, the free tools — comes from that real business, not market research.

Common questions

Quick answers

Is FlipperHelper really free?

Yes. Everything in the app today — item tracking, expenses, profit calculation, multi-currency, backups, Google Sheets export, offline mode — is free with no ads, and stays free. We're building a paid Pro tier on top (web portal, eBay connect) for resellers who want more automation.

Does it work offline at the market?

Yes — FlipperHelper is offline-first. Your data is stored on your device, so you can log finds, mark listings and record sales with no internet. Sync catches up when you're back online — and Pro's cloud sync will follow the same rule.

How is this different from Vendoo?

Different category. Vendoo posts your listings to multiple marketplaces — FlipperHelper tracks each item's path and your real profit after every expense. Many resellers use a cross-lister and a tracker together. See the full comparison for an honest breakdown.

What platforms can I track listings on?

Sixteen: eBay, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy, Gumtree, Shpock, Preloved, Nextdoor, Grailed, ShopThing, OfferUp, Craigslist and ThredUp — plus offline sales at boots and fairs.

Can I switch between iPhone and Android without losing data?

Yes. Both apps share the same backup format — a single ZIP with your database and every photo. Export on one platform, import on the other. Switching phones is a three-step, five-minute job.

Will it help with my tax records?

It keeps a dated record of every purchase, sale and expense, and exports the lot to Google Sheets — a solid foundation for the HMRC trading allowance and Self Assessment. Our blog covers how UK resellers use it for tax season.
