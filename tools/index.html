Free Reseller Tools

In-browser tools for resellers. No signup. No ads. No data leaves your device.

Reselling throws up the same handful of awkward questions over and over: what is this hallmark, what date is this piece, what will eBay take after fees, is this even worth listing. Most answers are buried in forum threads or behind sign-up walls. These tools are the quick, honest answers — built for the way UK resellers actually work: at a car boot in the rain, in a charity shop queue, or sorting a haul on the kitchen table. Everything runs in your browser, so it works on your phone and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

They’re free because they’re the same problems we hit sourcing and listing every week. If a tool earns its place by genuinely saving you time, it stays; if it’s just filler, we don’t ship it.
