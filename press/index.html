The one-liner

What is FlipperHelper?

FlipperHelper is a free app for iOS and Android for people who buy secondhand items at car boot sales, charity shops, and flea markets, then resell them on platforms like eBay, Vinted, and Facebook Marketplace. It tracks every item from purchase through listing to sale and calculates your real profit after all expenses.

Built by Oleksandr Prudnikov, a Ukrainian developer living in Wandsworth, South West London. He made it for his wife after she started reselling and had no way to track what she was buying, where she was listing it, and whether she was actually making money.

The short version

Core Features

  • Photo-first item tracking with automatic SKU generation
  • Track listings across 16 selling platforms with per-platform dates
  • Automatic profit calculation per item
  • Expense tracking: entry fees, transport (car, bus, train, taxi, van, bike rental), and custom expenses
  • Multi-currency support — 6 currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, NZD) with automatic daily exchange rates
  • Market management with GPS auto-detection
  • Money Flow dashboard with weekly, monthly, and custom range revenue, profit, and expense breakdown
  • Hauls — group items and expenses by sourcing trip with per-trip profit tracking
  • 5 share card types (Flip Card, Monthly Recap, Top Flips, Daily Haul, My Stock) for social sharing
  • Automatic backup enabled by default — iCloud on iPhone, folder backup on Android
  • Google Drive photo sync and CSV export to Google Sheets (drive.file scope only)
  • Full data backup and restore as ZIP archive
  • Listing reminders via configurable push notifications
  • Works completely offline — no account required
