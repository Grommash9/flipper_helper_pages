Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Introduction

FlipperHelper ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Oleksandr Prudnikov, a sole trader registered in the United Kingdom. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("FlipperHelper" or the "App").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This policy applies to users in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, Australia, and all other regions where the App is available.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Purchase Records: Item descriptions, purchase prices, sale prices, dates, and associated market/seller names that you enter into the App.
  • Photos: Images you take or select for your inventory items.
  • Market Information: Names and optional location coordinates of markets you add.
  • Seller Information: Names of sellers you choose to track.
  • Expense Records: Entry fees and transport expenses you log.
  • App Settings: Your preferences such as enabled selling platforms, notification settings, and display options.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: With your permission, we access your device location to suggest nearby markets. Your device location is used only on your device and is not sent to our servers. If you save a location for a market and use cloud sync, the market's coordinates sync with your records.
  • Usage Analytics: We record how the App is used. For example: when the App is opened, which screens you view, when you add or sell an item, which selling platforms you mark items as listed on, how long the App takes to start, and when you buy Pro. Each event includes the app version, device model, and operating system version. Usage analytics never include the contents of your records: no item descriptions, photos, prices, market names, or seller names.
  • Installation ID and Account ID: When the App is first opened, it creates a random installation ID. This ID is not your device's hardware ID or advertising identifier. A new one is created if you reinstall the App. Usage analytics include the installation ID and, if you are signed in to a FlipperHelper account, your account ID. This means usage analytics are linked to your account.
  • Users in the European Economic Area: If your device's region setting is a country in the European Economic Area (the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway), the App sends usage analytics without the installation ID and without your account ID. TelemetryDeck still creates its own pseudonymous identifier (see "Third-Party Services").
  • Crash and Error Diagnostics: If you are signed in, the App also sends diagnostic reports. They cover crashes (the error type and the code locations involved, without error messages), failed requests to our servers, sync failures, and photo upload and download statistics (number of photos, size, duration, and network type such as Wi-Fi or cellular). Diagnostic reports include the installation ID and your account ID, so we can find and fix technical faults.
  • What We Do Not Collect: We do not store your IP address or your device location with usage analytics or diagnostic reports. The App does not collect your device's advertising identifier. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not use usage analytics for advertising, so no tracking permission prompt is shown.

Information from Third-Party Services

  • Google Account: If you choose to connect Google Drive for export and photo sync, we access your Google email address and name to identify your account. We use the limited drive.file scope, which only allows access to files created by FlipperHelper—not your other Google Drive files.

FlipperHelper Account and Pro (Optional Cloud Sync)

Signing in is optional. Cloud sync is available with FlipperHelper Pro, or when you are a member of a team whose owner has Pro. If you create a FlipperHelper account, we additionally collect:

  • Account ID and Contact Details: A unique account ID and, if your sign-in provider shares them, your email address and name, provided by Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google.
  • Reselling Records (Cloud Sync): The purchase, sale, market, seller, expense, and haul records you choose to sync, so they are available across your devices. Each synced change records which account and which installation made it, with a short device description such as "iPhone · iOS 18.2".
  • Photos (Cloud Sync): The item photos you sync, so they are available across your devices.
  • Teams (Optional): If you create or join a team, the team's name, its members, and the email addresses of the people you invite.
  • eBay Account Connection (Optional): If you connect your eBay account, we store an encrypted authorization token and your eBay username/user ID. We receive details of your own eBay listings and sales—such as title, SKU, price, fees, and dates—from eBay's notifications and APIs, so new activity is added to your records automatically. To support and debug this connection, we also keep a complete copy of each request we send to eBay's APIs and each response we receive, for 90 days. Authorization tokens and keys are removed from these copies. The copies can include personal data of your buyers that eBay returns with an order, such as their names and addresses.
  • Subscription Status: Your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription status, used to confirm you have an active Pro subscription.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the App's functionality:

  • Core Features: To track your purchases, listings, sales, and calculate profits.
  • Photo Backup: To sync your item photos to your Google Drive (if enabled).
  • Data Export: To generate spreadsheets of your data in your Google Drive (when you request it).
  • Notifications: To send local reminders about unlisted items (if enabled).
  • Market Suggestions: To suggest nearby markets based on your location (if permitted).
  • Cloud Sync (Optional): To sync your records and photos across your devices and with your team, to add your eBay listings and sales to your records, and to check your Pro subscription.
  • Usage Analytics: To understand how the App is used—such as active users, which features are used, and how fast the App starts—so we can improve the App.
  • Diagnostics: To find and fix crashes, failed requests, and sync problems.
  • eBay Support (Optional): To investigate problems with your eBay connection, using the copies of eBay requests and responses described above.
  • Account Emails: If you create an account, we send a welcome email confirming your registration, and may occasionally send product news and updates by email. You can unsubscribe from news emails at any time using the link included in every such email; this never affects account-related emails such as a data export or an account-deletion confirmation.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than those listed above.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All your records are stored locally on your device in the App's private storage. This includes:

  • All purchase, sale, and expense records
  • Market and seller information
  • Photos (original copies)
  • App settings and preferences

Google Drive (Optional)

If you enable Google Drive sync:

  • Photos are uploaded to a folder called FlipperHelper_App/images/ in your Google Drive.
  • Exported spreadsheets are saved to your Google Drive.
  • This data is protected by your Google account security.

Your Records Without Cloud Sync

If you do not use cloud sync, your records—inventory, photos, prices, markets, and sellers—are not stored on our servers. The only copy of your records that leaves your device goes to your own Google Drive, if you enable it. You control that copy and can disconnect Google Drive at any time. The App does send usage analytics, and if you are signed in, your account ID and diagnostic reports, as described in "Information Collected Automatically" above.

FlipperHelper Account and Pro (Cloud Storage)

If you create a FlipperHelper account, the information listed under "FlipperHelper Account and Pro (Optional Cloud Sync)" above, including your synced photos, is stored on our behalf using Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure, rather than only on your device, so it can sync across your devices. This data is encrypted in transit, and sensitive fields such as your eBay authorization token are also encrypted at rest. Synced photos are kept in private storage that is encrypted at rest.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Our servers run on AWS. Account data, synced records, and synced photos are stored in the United States (N. Virginia), with copies in the European Union (Frankfurt) and Australia (Sydney). Usage analytics that reach our servers are stored in the United States (N. Virginia). Diagnostic reports and server logs are kept in the AWS region that handled the request: the United States, the European Union, or Australia. TelemetryDeck processes the usage analytics it receives under its own privacy policy (see "Third-Party Services").

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Your data only leaves your device in these circumstances:

  • Google Drive Sync: When you choose to back up photos or export data to your own Google Drive account.
  • Device Backups: If you back up your iPhone via iCloud or iTunes, App data may be included in those backups (controlled by Apple).
  • Usage Analytics: Usage analytics are sent to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service, and, if you are signed in, to our own servers on AWS (see "Information Collected Automatically" and "Third-Party Services").
  • eBay (Pro, Optional): If you connect your eBay account, we exchange authorization data with eBay's API to receive details about your own listings and sales. We do not share your data with eBay beyond what is required to maintain that connection.
  • Amazon Web Services: If you are signed in, your account data, usage analytics, and diagnostic reports—and with cloud sync, your synced records and photos—are hosted on AWS infrastructure, which processes it on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Account Emails: We send account emails through Microsoft 365, which processes your email address and the content of the email on our behalf.
  • Team Members (Optional): If you join a team, the records and photos you sync to that team are visible to its members.

Your Rights

All Users

You can:

  • Access your data: All your records are visible within the App. To get a copy of the data we hold on our servers, see below.
  • Edit your data: Modify any records directly in the App.
  • Delete your data: Delete individual items, or use Settings → Danger zone → Delete all data. If you are signed in, this also deletes your account.
  • Export your data: Export all data to Google Sheets at any time.
  • Disconnect Google: Revoke Google Drive access in the App's Settings.
  • Turn off usage analytics: Turn off usage analytics in the App's Settings at any time. The App then stops sending usage analytics to TelemetryDeck and to our servers. Crash and error diagnostics that we need to detect technical faults can continue while you are signed in.

Users with a FlipperHelper Account

If you have a FlipperHelper account, you can additionally:

  • Export your account data: Request a copy of everything we hold about your account by emailing support@flipperhelper.app; we will send it to the email address on file.
  • Delete your account: In the App, go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete all data, or email us. We delete your account, the teams you own, your synced records and photos, your eBay connection, the stored copies of eBay requests and responses, and the usage analytics linked to your account. We will confirm by email once your account has been deleted. Some data stays for a limited time or with other people, as described in "Data Retention".
  • Unsubscribe from news emails: Use the unsubscribe link included in any news/product-update email at any time.

UK and EEA Users (UK GDPR and EU GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have additional rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, as described above. Records you keep without cloud sync are stored only on your device.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate data (available directly in the App).
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your records directly in the App. To delete your account and the data linked to it, use Settings → Danger zone → Delete all data, or email us, as described above.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your data in a common format (CSV/Google Sheets export available). For an account, request an export as described above.
  • Right to Object: Object to how we process your data. You can turn off usage analytics in the App's Settings at any time, or email us.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for location access or Google Drive sync at any time in Settings.
  • Right to Complain: Complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in the EEA, the data protection authority of your country.

If your device's region setting is a country in the European Economic Area, usage analytics are sent without the installation ID and without your account ID, as described in "Information Collected Automatically".

For GDPR inquiries, contact us at support@flipperhelper.app.

United States Users

Wherever you live in the United States, you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, and ask us to delete it, by emailing support@flipperhelper.app. California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected.
  • Delete personal information.
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your data).
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

Australian Users

You can ask for access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@flipperhelper.app. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). Some of your data is stored outside Australia, in the United States and the European Union, as described in "Where Your Data Is Stored".

Data Retention

If you do not use cloud sync, your records remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App, and we do not retain any copies of them on our servers.

If you use Google Drive export:

  • Photos and exports remain in your Google Drive until you delete them.
  • Disconnecting Google Drive from the App does not automatically delete files already uploaded—you can manage these directly in Google Drive.

If you have a FlipperHelper account, your account, synced records, and synced photos are retained on our servers for as long as your account remains active, and are permanently deleted when you delete your account as described in "Your Rights" above, with these exceptions:

  • Teams owned by someone else: If you are a member of a team that someone else owns, the records and photos you synced to that team stay with that team. They still show which installation made each change, with its device description.
  • Record of the deletion: We keep a one-way hash of your account ID to record that the account was deleted.

Other data is kept as follows:

  • Usage analytics on our servers: Kept indefinitely. When you delete your account, we delete the usage analytics linked to it within 30 days.
  • Diagnostic reports and server logs: Deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Copies of eBay requests and responses: Deleted after 90 days, or earlier when you delete your account.
  • Usage analytics at TelemetryDeck: Kept by TelemetryDeck under its own retention policy. They are not deleted when you delete your account.

Children's Privacy

FlipperHelper is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Third-Party Services

Google Drive API

FlipperHelper uses the Google Drive API to:

  • Upload item photos to your Google Drive.
  • Create and update spreadsheets with your exported data.

We request only the drive.file scope, which limits our access to files created by the App. We cannot access your other Google Drive files. Google's privacy policy applies to data stored in Google Drive: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google API Services User Data Policy

FlipperHelper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only use Google Drive access to provide the App's photo sync and data export features.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising purposes.
  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not use Google user data for any purpose other than providing the App's functionality.

TelemetryDeck (Analytics)

FlipperHelper uses TelemetryDeck for usage analytics. The App sends TelemetryDeck some of the usage analytics described in "Information Collected Automatically". Outside the European Economic Area, they include the installation ID and, if you are signed in, your account ID. TelemetryDeck's software adds technical details: device model, operating system version, screen size, language, region and time zone settings, accessibility settings, and usage counters such as the date of first use and the number of sessions. TelemetryDeck also creates its own pseudonymous identifier to count active users. On iPhone, it is a hash of the identifier for vendor, an ID that Apple gives to apps from the same developer on one device. On Android, it is a random ID that TelemetryDeck's software stores with the App. The App does not send TelemetryDeck your device's advertising identifier. TelemetryDeck's privacy policy: https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/

Apple Services

The App may interact with standard Apple services (Photos library, Location Services, Notifications). Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Amazon Web Services (Cloud Infrastructure)

If you are signed in, your account data, usage analytics, and diagnostic reports—and with cloud sync, your synced records and photos—are hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure. AWS acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes. AWS's privacy policy: https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/

eBay (Pro, Optional)

If you connect your eBay account, FlipperHelper uses eBay's APIs to identify your account and receive details about your own listings and sales. eBay's privacy policy: https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-privacy-notice-privacy-policy

Microsoft 365 (Account Emails)

We send account emails, such as the welcome email and the account-deletion confirmation, through Microsoft 365. Microsoft processes your email address and the content of the email on our behalf. Microsoft's privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
