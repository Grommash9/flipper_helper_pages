FlipperHelper vs Vendoo: Which Reseller App?

Last verified: 2026-05-06

Quick verdict

These two apps solve different problems. FlipperHelper is a inventory and profit tracker for resellers; Vendoo is a cross-lister for online marketplaces. A tracker tells you whether you're making money; a cross-lister helps you list faster across multiple platforms.

  • Choose FlipperHelper if: UK resellers sourcing at car boots, charity shops, antiques fairs, brocantes; multi-platform sellers; anyone who needs offline-first entry and per-trip profit grouping.
  • Choose Vendoo if: Resellers who list on 3+ platforms (eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace) and spend hours copying the same listing across them.
  • Many resellers use both — a tracker for the money side, a cross-lister for the listing side.

What FlipperHelper does well

  • Real profit calculation after entry fees, transport, packaging, and platform fees
  • Hauls — group items by sourcing trip and track per-trip profitability over time
  • Offline-first — works at 6am car boots with no signal
  • Multi-currency (useful for sourcing in France or Belgium)
  • 16 selling platforms tracked
  • Two backup paths: automatic backup (iCloud on iPhone, folder on Android) and ZIP export; plus optional Google Drive / Sheets export
  • Free, no ads, no account required

FlipperHelper limitations

  • Mobile only — iOS and Android, no web app
  • No marketplace API yet (manual sale-marking until eBay tier ships)
  • No cross-listing
  • No built-in barcode scanner / eBay sold-price lookup
  • Newer app (launched March 2026)

What Vendoo does well

  • Create a listing once, push to multiple marketplaces
  • Auto-delists from other platforms when an item sells
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
  • Tiered paid plans from $8.99 to $69.99 per month

Vendoo limitations

  • Not a profit tracker — no expense tracking, no mileage, no entry fees, no real profit calculation
  • Free tier limited to 3 new listings per month
  • Different problem space from inventory trackers
