Quick verdict

Quick verdict

These two apps solve different problems. Flippd is a inventory and profit tracker with price research; Vendoo is a cross-lister for online marketplaces. A tracker tells you whether you're making money; a cross-lister helps you list faster across multiple platforms.

  • Choose Flippd if: Resellers who need multi-platform support (iOS / Android / web), built-in barcode scanning, or eBay sold-price lookup before buying.
  • Choose Vendoo if: Resellers who list on 3+ platforms (eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace) and spend hours copying the same listing across them.
  • Many resellers use both — a tracker for the money side, a cross-lister for the listing side.

Common questions

FAQ

What is the difference between Flippd and Vendoo?

Flippd and Vendoo solve different problems. Flippd is a inventory and profit tracker with price research; Vendoo is a cross-lister for online marketplaces. A tracker answers "Am I making money?"; a cross-lister answers "How do I list faster?". Many resellers use one of each.

Flippd or Vendoo — which should I use?

Flippd is best for: Resellers who need multi-platform support (iOS / Android / web), built-in barcode scanning, or eBay sold-price lookup before buying. Vendoo is best for: Resellers who list on 3+ platforms (eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace) and spend hours copying the same listing across them. The right choice depends on your sourcing pattern, the platforms you sell on, and whether you want a free tool or a subscription.

What does Flippd cost?

Flippd: Free up to 20 items lifetime. Paid: $9.99/month flat (unlimited items).

What does Vendoo cost?

Vendoo: Free tier: 3 new listings per month. Paid: $8.99/month (25 listings) to $69.99/month (unlimited).
