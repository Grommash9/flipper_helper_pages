Flippd vs Vendoo: Which Reseller App?

Last verified: 2026-05-06

Quick verdict

These two apps solve different problems. Flippd is a inventory and profit tracker with price research; Vendoo is a cross-lister for online marketplaces. A tracker tells you whether you're making money; a cross-lister helps you list faster across multiple platforms.

  • Choose Flippd if: Resellers who need multi-platform support (iOS / Android / web), built-in barcode scanning, or eBay sold-price lookup before buying.
  • Choose Vendoo if: Resellers who list on 3+ platforms (eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace) and spend hours copying the same listing across them.
  • Many resellers use both — a tracker for the money side, a cross-lister for the listing side.

What Flippd does well

  • Built-in barcode scanning
  • Image-based eBay sold-price lookup
  • QR label printing for inventory organisation
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
  • Inventory and profit tracking core
  • Flat-rate pricing, unlimited items on paid tier

Flippd limitations

  • Free tier capped at 20 items lifetime (not monthly)
  • Subscription required for serious use
  • No cross-listing — same as FlipperHelper, it tracks only

What Vendoo does well

  • Create a listing once, push to multiple marketplaces
  • Auto-delists from other platforms when an item sells
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
  • Tiered paid plans from $8.99 to $69.99 per month

Vendoo limitations

  • Not a profit tracker — no expense tracking, no mileage, no entry fees, no real profit calculation
  • Free tier limited to 3 new listings per month
  • Different problem space from inventory trackers
