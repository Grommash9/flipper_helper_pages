FlipperHelper vs Flippd: Which Reseller App?

Last verified: 2026-05-06

Quick verdict

FlipperHelper and Flippd sit in the same category (inventory and profit tracker for resellerss) but differ in platforms, pricing, and feature focus.

  • Choose FlipperHelper if: UK resellers sourcing at car boots, charity shops, antiques fairs, brocantes; multi-platform sellers; anyone who needs offline-first entry and per-trip profit grouping.
  • Choose Flippd if: Resellers who need multi-platform support (iOS / Android / web), built-in barcode scanning, or eBay sold-price lookup before buying.

What FlipperHelper does well

  • Real profit calculation after entry fees, transport, packaging, and platform fees
  • Hauls — group items by sourcing trip and track per-trip profitability over time
  • Offline-first — works at 6am car boots with no signal
  • Multi-currency (useful for sourcing in France or Belgium)
  • 16 selling platforms tracked
  • Two backup paths: automatic backup (iCloud on iPhone, folder on Android) and ZIP export; plus optional Google Drive / Sheets export
  • Free, no ads, no account required

FlipperHelper limitations

  • Mobile only — iOS and Android, no web app
  • No marketplace API yet (manual sale-marking until eBay tier ships)
  • No cross-listing
  • No built-in barcode scanner / eBay sold-price lookup
  • Newer app (launched March 2026)

What Flippd does well

  • Built-in barcode scanning
  • Image-based eBay sold-price lookup
  • QR label printing for inventory organisation
  • Available on iOS, Android, and web
  • Inventory and profit tracking core
  • Flat-rate pricing, unlimited items on paid tier

Flippd limitations

  • Free tier capped at 20 items lifetime (not monthly)
  • Subscription required for serious use
  • No cross-listing — same as FlipperHelper, it tracks only
