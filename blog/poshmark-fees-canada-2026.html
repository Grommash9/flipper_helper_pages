Poshmark Fees Canada 2026 — What You Actually Keep

14 June 2026 Fees 7 min read

Poshmark Canada keeps a flat $3.95 CAD on every sale under $20 CAD, and 20% of every sale of $20 CAD or more. Poshmark also deducts GST/HST on its fee from your earnings. The rate depends on your province. The buyer pays shipping: $12.49 CAD plus taxes for Canada Post Home Delivery. On a $50 CAD sale by an Ontario seller, you keep $38.70 before you subtract what you paid for the item.

This article explains the fee structure and the GST/HST on Poshmark fees. It gives worked CAD examples for common sale prices. Poshmark’s Canada Fee Policy took effect on 18 December 2023. Poshmark still shows it as the current version.

Update, 13 November 2026: an earlier version of this page had wrong figures for the $20 rule, shipping and other platforms. We checked every fee figure below against Poshmark Canada, eBay, Kijiji, Mercari and CRA pages on 13 November 2026.

Sources: Poshmark Canada Fee Policy; Poshmark Help, What are the shipping rates on Poshmark?

How much does Poshmark Canada take? (2026 fee structure)

Sale price (CAD)Poshmark commissionEffective fee %
$5 to $19.99 ($5 is the minimum listing price)$3.95 flat19.76% to 79% (higher at lower prices)
$20 and above20% of the sale20%

Sources: Poshmark Help, What are the fees for selling on Poshmark?; Poshmark Help, Why is there a commission fee?; Poshmark Canada Fee Policy

The fee rule changes at $20. A $19.99 sale loses $3.95 (19.76%). A $20 sale loses $4.00 (20%). These two results are almost the same. But a $19 sale loses 20.79%, and a $15 sale loses 26.3%. A $5 sale, at the lowest listing price Poshmark allows, loses 79%. Sellers who specialise in low-value items pay the highest percentage. These percentages do not include the GST/HST on the fee.

How much you keep on a $50 CAD Poshmark sale (worked example)

LineAmount (CAD)
Item sold$50.00
Shipping (the buyer pays $12.49 plus taxes; you get a prepaid label)$0 from you
Poshmark commission (20% of $50)−$10.00
GST/HST on commission (Ontario, 13%)−$1.30
Seller's Poshmark balance$38.70 (Ontario example: $50 − $10 commission − $1.30 HST = $38.70)

This balance does not include what you paid for the item, packaging, or any transport. For example, you bought the item at Value Village for $5 and packaged it in a $2 mailer. Your real profit is then $31.70. If you subtract only the $5 cost from the $50 price, you get $45. That figure ignores the $10 commission and the $1.30 HST.

Sources: Poshmark Help, What are the shipping rates on Poshmark?; Poshmark Help, How to ship a sale; CRA, GST/HST rates

Worked example: $15 CAD sale (under the $20 threshold)

  • Item sold: $15
  • Poshmark commission: $3.95 flat
  • GST/HST on commission (Ontario): $0.51
  • Net to seller: $10.54
  • Effective fee (including HST): 29.7%

For example, you bought the item at a Toronto yard sale for $1 and spent $1.50 on packaging. Your real profit is then $8.04.

Shipping discounts — who pays

You can offer buyers a shipping discount. You can set it on a listing, or you can include it in an offer, for example an Offer to Likers. Every Offer to Likers must include a shipping discount.

You pay for the discount, not Poshmark. Poshmark’s help page says: “Listing shipping discounts are deducted from a seller’s net earnings.” Poshmark also deducts GST/HST on the discount. The rate depends on the buyer’s province. If the buyer counters your Offer to Likers, the shipping discount in that offer no longer applies.

Sources: Poshmark Help, What is a listing shipping discount?; Poshmark Help, How to send an offer to likers

Does Poshmark charge GST/HST on its commission? (what sellers need to know)

Yes. Poshmark’s help page says: “All orders include a GST/HST on Poshmark fees that are deducted from the seller’s earnings.” The rate depends on your province. These are the CRA GST/HST rates:

  • Ontario: 13% HST
  • New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island: 15% HST
  • Nova Scotia: 14% HST (since 1 April 2025)
  • Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Quebec, Saskatchewan, Yukon, Northwest Territories, Nunavut: 5% GST

In British Columbia, Poshmark also charges PST on its fee to individual sellers when the buyer is also in B.C. Poshmark’s fee pages name GST/HST on the fee. They do not mention QST on the fee for Quebec sellers.

Poshmark also collects the applicable GST/HST, QST and PST on the order price and on the buyer’s shipping fee. The rates depend on the buyer’s province and the shipping destination. Poshmark remits these taxes. You pay GST/HST on any shipping discount you give and on any overweight label.

For Ontario sellers in the $50 CAD example, the HST on the fee is $1.30 per sale. You must register for the GST/HST when your revenues from taxable supplies, plus those of your associates, go over $30,000. This limit applies to a single calendar quarter or to the last four consecutive calendar quarters. If you are registered, you may be able to claim the GST/HST on Poshmark fees as an input tax credit (ITC). If you are not registered, you cannot claim ITCs, so this tax is a cost to you.

Sources: Poshmark Help, Why is there a commission fee?; Poshmark Help, How is tax calculated on Poshmark?; Poshmark Help, How to get an overweight shipping label; CRA, GST/HST rates; CRA, RC4022 General Information for GST/HST Registrants; CRA, Input tax credits

Sources: Poshmark Canada Fee Policy; eBay.ca, Selling fees; Kijiji, Category Pricing Overview | Listing Fees; Mercari Help, Shipping labels, tracking, and carrier options

For eBay, the $50 example adds 13% Ontario HST to get a total amount of the sale of $56.50. The fee is 13.6% of $56.50 plus $0.40. eBay says that GST or a similar tax “might apply in addition to your eBay fees”. If 13% HST applies to the eBay fee, the total is $9.13 (18.3%). Because the buyer pays no shipping, you also pay the postage. eBay gives 250 free listings per month, then charges $0.30 per listing.

Mercari is not in this table. Mercari’s help centre says: “Mercari ships to all 50 states and the District of Columbia.”

In this $50 example, Poshmark Canada takes more than eBay Canada. Kijiji charges no posting fee for clothing listings. Under $20, the flat $3.95 Poshmark fee is a large part of the price: 26.3% of a $15 sale.

FAQ

Will Poshmark suspend me for arranging off-platform sales?

It can. Poshmark says that buying or selling a Poshmark listing “using any means other than Poshmark checkout is considered an offline transaction and is prohibited.” Poshmark’s terms say that it “may suspend or terminate a User’s Account at our discretion”. Posh Protect does not cover offline transactions, so Poshmark cannot refund the buyer if something goes wrong.

Why is Poshmark Canada different from Poshmark US?

Poshmark expanded into Canada in May 2019, as the company’s first international launch. Poshmark Canada states its fees in Canadian dollars. It ships only within Canada and its territories. It does not currently support shipping outside Canada, so you cannot ship to US buyers from a Poshmark Canada closet.

Is the $12.49 CAD shipping the same for every package?

The buyer pays $12.49 CAD plus taxes for Canada Post Home Delivery. Where it is available, Purolator Pick Up costs the buyer $6.99 CAD plus taxes. The Poshmark label covers packages up to 2.5 kg at no extra cost. For a heavier package, you need a new label. Poshmark deducts a label upgrade fee of $1 to $5 CAD, plus GST/HST on shipping, from your earnings. The maximum weight is 5 kg.

How does Poshmark Canada pay me?

Poshmark releases your earnings when the buyer accepts the order. If the buyer opens no return case within 3 days of delivery, Poshmark releases the payment automatically. Direct deposit to your bank account has no fee and usually takes 1–3 business days. PayPal costs $0.50 CAD, and Visa Instant Transfer costs $2.00 CAD. Poshmark does not support cheque payouts in Canada.

What happens if a buyer requests a return?

Poshmark says: “All sales are final on Poshmark.” But the buyer has 72 hours after delivery to open a return case. The buyer cannot open a case after accepting the order. Poshmark lists these valid reasons: undisclosed damage, incorrect or missing item, item not as described, item not authentic, and order never shipped or received. Poshmark does not offer returns for fit issues, a change of mind, or delayed delivery. If Poshmark approves the return, it sends the buyer a prepaid label to send the item back to you. When the item reaches you, Poshmark gives the buyer a full refund, including shipping and tax.
