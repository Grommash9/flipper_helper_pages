Poshmark Fees Canada 2026 — What You Actually Keep

14 June 2026 Fees 7 min read

Poshmark Canada keeps $3.95 CAD on every sale under $20 CAD, and 20% of every sale at $20 CAD or above. The buyer pays shipping ($13.49 CAD standard via Canada Post Expedited Parcel). Poshmark takes the 20% from the item price, not from shipping. On a $50 CAD sale, you take home $40 CAD before you subtract what you paid for the item.

This article explains the exact fee structure and the GST/HST treatment that Canadian sellers face. It also covers what is changing in 2026 and gives worked CAD examples for the most common sale prices. It is based on Poshmark Canada's official fee page and on current discussions on r/poshmarkcanada.

How much does Poshmark Canada take? (2026 fee structure)

Sale price (CAD)Poshmark commissionEffective fee %
Under $20$3.95 flat20–100% (higher at lower prices)
$20$3.95 flat19.75%
$20.01 and above20% of item price20% exactly

The fee rule changes at $20. A $19.99 sale loses $3.95 (19.76%). A $20.01 sale loses $4.00 (19.99%). These two results are almost the same. But a $19 sale loses 20.79%, and a $15 sale loses 26.3%. Sellers who specialise in low-value items pay the highest percentage.

This balance does not include what you paid for the item, packaging, or any transport. For example, you bought the item at Value Village for $5 and packaged it in a $2 mailer. Your real profit is then $31.70. A common reseller mistake is to call that "$45 profit". These resellers forget the 20% Poshmark commission and the GST.

Worked example: $15 CAD sale (the worst case)

  • Item sold: $15
  • Poshmark commission: $3.95 flat
  • GST/HST on commission (Ontario): $0.51
  • Net to seller: $10.54
  • Effective fee: 29.7%

For example, you bought the item at a Toronto yard sale for $1 and spent $1.50 on packaging. Your real profit is then $8.04. Most low-price sellers don't do this per-item maths. The thread of complaints on r/poshmark (327 upvotes, "Poshmark is a resell site, not a boutique") shows this. Many of the "I quit Poshmark" posts come from low-value sellers who saw what their real net was.

The $4.49 shipping discount — when it applies

Poshmark periodically lets sellers offer discounted shipping of $4.49 CAD instead of the default $13.49. It is a promotion that Poshmark pays for. You apply it through the "Send Offer to Likers" flow with the discount checkbox. The buyer sees $4.49 shipping instead of $13.49, and Poshmark pays the difference.

One r/poshmarkcanada thread (14 upvotes, 21 comments) reported a bug: the $4.49 discount sometimes didn't apply at checkout. "I sent out a handful of offers to likers this morning 30% off with $9.99 shipping. I got a few sales and then a message saying that the $4.49 shipping wasn't applying for my buyer when they went to check out." The fix has worked only some of the time. If you offer discounted shipping, take a screenshot of the offer before you send it. This gives you proof of the agreement.

Does Poshmark charge GST/HST on its commission? (what sellers need to know)

Poshmark Canada collects GST/HST on its commission only (not on buyer-paid shipping). The rate depends on your province:

  • Ontario: 13% HST on commission
  • Quebec: 5% GST + 9.975% QST on commission
  • BC, MB, SK: 5% GST on commission (provincial PST separate)
  • Alberta, Yukon, NT, Nunavut: 5% GST on commission
  • Atlantic provinces: 15% HST on commission

For Ontario sellers in the $50 CAD example, that's an extra $1.30 per sale. GST/HST registration is mandatory above $30,000 CAD of taxable supplies in a rolling 12 months. If you're GST/HST-registered, you may be able to claim this tax as an input tax credit. Below the registration threshold, it is only a cost. Track it from day one, because it is hard to reconstruct the amount at tax time.

Poshmark Canada is the most expensive of the major Canadian platforms on a per-sale fee basis. The trade-off is visibility. The social feed and Posh Parties give your items visibility that the other platforms don't give. For mid-range clothing ($30–$150), this extra visibility is often worth the fee. For low-value items ($5–$20), the flat $3.95 fee makes Mercari or Kijiji cheaper.

FAQ

Will Poshmark suspend me for arranging off-platform sales?

Yes. Poshmark's terms forbid using the platform to arrange off-platform transactions. Poshmark suspends accounts that it finds doing this. Off-platform sales also lose Poshmark's buyer protection. This puts both sides at risk of fraud.

Why is Poshmark Canada different from Poshmark US?

Poshmark Canada launched as a separate marketplace in 2019 with its own seller base, listings, and currency (CAD). The percentages are the same, but the tax treatment and the shipping infrastructure are different. You cannot sell to US buyers directly from your Poshmark Canada closet.

Is the $13.49 CAD shipping the same for every package?

It's a flat rate for most domestic Canada Post Expedited Parcel packages up to a certain weight (typically 2.5 kg). For oversize items, Poshmark adds an extra fee at checkout. Shipping heavy items (over 2.5 kg) quickly becomes expensive.

Does Poshmark pay me in CAD?

Yes. Your Poshmark Canada earnings go to your Canadian bank account in CAD. You don't need currency conversion unless you set up a separate US Poshmark account.

What happens if a buyer requests a return?

Poshmark allows returns only for misrepresented items (wrong size from what was listed, undisclosed damage, fake brand). If the return is accepted, the buyer ships the item back at Poshmark's cost. Your sale is then reversed, and your commission is refunded to you. Buyer's remorse is not a valid return reason on Poshmark. This rule is unusually seller-friendly compared to eBay.
