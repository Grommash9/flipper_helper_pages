FlipperHelper vs Flippd vs Vendoo: Which Do You Need?

3 April 2026 Comparison 7 min read

These three apps solve fundamentally different problems. FlipperHelper and Flippd are inventory and profit trackers — they tell you whether you're actually making money. Vendoo is a cross-lister — it helps you push listings to multiple platforms at once.

Comparing them directly is like comparing a calculator to a printer. You might need one, the other, or both. Here's an honest breakdown to help you decide.

"I wasted months using a cross-lister thinking it would track my profit. It didn't. Once I understood the difference between a tracker and a cross-lister, everything clicked." — Oleksandr Prudnikov, FlipperHelper developer

What is the difference between a tracker and a cross-lister?

A tracker answers "Am I making money?" A cross-lister answers "How do I list faster?" These are two separate problems that most resellers face, and no single app solves both well.

  • Trackers (FlipperHelper, Flippd) — log purchases, track expenses (entry fees, transport, packaging), record sales, and calculate your real profit after all costs
  • Cross-listers (Vendoo) — create a listing once and push it to multiple selling platforms (eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, etc.), detect when something sells, and auto-delist from other platforms

A tracker won't create listings for you. A cross-lister won't tell you if your car boot trip was profitable. Understanding this distinction saves you from choosing the wrong tool.

What does FlipperHelper do well?

FlipperHelper is built for resellers who source at markets, car boot sales, and charity shops. It covers the full journey from picking up an item to seeing your real profit after every expense. It's free to download with no ads.

  • Photo capture at purchase — snap and log in under 10 seconds, right at the stall
  • 16 selling platforms — track where items are listed (eBay, Vinted, Facebook, Depop, Shpock, etc.)
  • Full expense tracking — entry fees, 6 transport types, packaging, and other costs. Smart entry fee suggestions remember previous amounts per market
  • Automatic profit calculation — real profit after ALL expenses, not just buy vs. sell
  • Hauls (sourcing trip tracking) — group items and expenses by trip dates and track per-trip profitability as items sell over time
  • Stock value tracking — see how much money is invested in unsold inventory, with items grouped by purchase date
  • Top Flips & share cards — see your most profitable sales and share them as visual cards to social media
  • Money Flow dashboard — view revenue, costs, profit, and stock investment by week, month, or custom date range
  • Market and seller tracking — GPS auto-detection, per-market analytics
  • Works offline — essential for markets with no phone signal
  • Automatic backup + Google Drive — automatic backup (iCloud on iPhone, folder on Android), image sync and Sheets export to Google Drive. Data survives even if the app is deleted
  • Multi-currency support — daily exchange rates for cross-border selling
  • Search — find items by name or SKU across your entire inventory

What are FlipperHelper's limitations?

  • No web app — mobile only (iOS and Android)
  • No cross-listing — you mark listings manually, not push them to platforms
  • No barcode scanning or eBay price lookup
  • Newer app with a smaller community

Price: Free to download, no ads.

What does Flippd do well?

Flippd is an inventory and profit tracker with built-in price research tools. It focuses on helping resellers know their numbers and find good buys through barcode scanning and image-based eBay lookups.

  • Barcode and image price lookup — scan an item or snap a photo to check eBay sold prices
  • GPS mileage tracking — automatic logging of driving distance for sourcing trips
  • Receipt scanning — AI-powered receipt reader to log purchases
  • "Death pile" alerts — notifications for items sitting too long unsold
  • QR label printing — generate labels for inventory organisation
  • Cross-platform — works on iOS, Android, and web

What are Flippd's limitations?

  • No cross-listing — same as FlipperHelper, it tracks but doesn't push listings
  • eBay sync is on a waitlist, not generally available
  • Free tier is very limited (lifetime cap of 20 items total, not monthly)
  • No offline mode mentioned in documentation

Price: $9.99/month (flat rate, unlimited items). Free tier: 20 items lifetime.

What does Vendoo do well?

Vendoo is a cross-listing tool that lets you create a listing once and push it to multiple marketplaces. If you spend hours copying the same listing across eBay, Vinted, Poshmark, and Facebook Marketplace, Vendoo is designed to solve that specific problem.

  • Cross-list to 10+ marketplaces — eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook, Vinted (added February 2026), and more
  • Sale detection — monitors platforms and detects when an item sells
  • Auto-delist — removes listings from other platforms when an item sells (requires desktop app running)
  • Bulk actions — relist, delist, and edit multiple items at once
  • Sales analytics — basic reporting on what sold and where

What are Vendoo's limitations?

  • Not a profit tracker — no expense tracking, no mileage, no entry fees, no real profit calculation
  • Auto-delist requires desktop — your computer needs to be on and connected for cross-platform delisting to work
  • US-focused — some marketplace integrations work better in the US than the UK
  • Per-platform forms still required — you fill in category-specific fields for each platform, so it's not truly one-click
  • No offline mode — requires internet connection

Price: Free tier: 3 new listings/month. Paid plans: $8.99/month (25 listings) to $69.99/month (unlimited).

Which app should you choose based on your reselling style?

Start with your biggest pain point, not the app with the most features. Here's a simple decision guide:

  • "I don't know if I'm actually making money" — you need a tracker. FlipperHelper if you're on iOS and source at markets (free, offline, expense tracking). Flippd if you want barcode price research or need Android/web access ($9.99/month)
  • "I spend hours copying the same listing to 4 platforms" — you need Vendoo. It won't track your profit, but it will save significant time on listing. Starts at $8.99/month
  • "I need both — profit tracking AND faster listing" — use a tracker alongside Vendoo. They don't overlap, so there's no duplication. Many serious resellers run both a tracker and a cross-lister
  • "I sell under 20 items a month on one platform" — you probably don't need a cross-lister at all. A tracker (or even a simple spreadsheet) is enough at this scale

Can you use FlipperHelper and Vendoo together?

Yes — they complement each other well because they solve different problems. A typical workflow for a reseller using both:

  1. At the car boot sale: snap a photo and log the purchase in FlipperHelper (tracks cost, market, entry fee)
  2. At home: create the listing in Vendoo and push it to eBay, Vinted, and Facebook in one go
  3. Mark the item as "listed" in FlipperHelper with the platforms you pushed to
  4. When it sells: Vendoo auto-delists from other platforms; you record the sale in FlipperHelper to see your real profit

FlipperHelper handles the money side (what did I spend, what did I make). Vendoo handles the listing side (get it in front of more buyers faster). Neither replaces the other.

What about FlipperHelper vs Flippd specifically?

Both are trackers, so they genuinely compete. The choice comes down to what matters most to you:

  • Choose FlipperHelper if: you source at markets and need offline mode, you want entry fee and transport expense tracking per trip, you want to track sourcing trip profitability with Hauls, you want social share cards for your best flips, you're on iOS, or you want a free tool with no item limits
  • Choose Flippd if: you need barcode/image price research at the point of purchase, you want GPS mileage tracking for tax purposes, you need Android or web access, or you want receipt scanning

FlipperHelper is stronger on the expense-tracking and market-sourcing side. Flippd is stronger on the price-research and mileage-tracking side. Both calculate profit, both track inventory, both support photos.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between FlipperHelper, Flippd, and Vendoo?

FlipperHelper and Flippd are inventory and profit trackers — they tell you what you bought, what you sold, and your real profit after expenses. Vendoo is a cross-lister — it pushes listings to multiple platforms at once. Trackers answer "Am I making money?" while cross-listers answer "How do I list faster?"

Is there a free alternative to Flippd?

FlipperHelper is a free alternative for inventory and profit tracking. It covers purchase logging with photos, multi-platform listing tracking, expense tracking, and automatic profit calculation. FlipperHelper is on iOS and Android, while Flippd also supports web.

Is there a free alternative to Vendoo?

Vendoo's free tier allows 3 new listings per month. There's no fully free cross-listing alternative with the same features. If your main need is profit tracking rather than cross-listing, FlipperHelper is free and handles that side. For cross-listing specifically, Vendoo's paid plans start at $8.99/month.

Do I need a tracker or a cross-lister for reselling?

If you don't know your real profit after expenses, start with a tracker (FlipperHelper or Flippd). If you list on 3+ platforms and spend hours duplicating listings, you need a cross-lister (Vendoo). Many serious resellers use one of each.
