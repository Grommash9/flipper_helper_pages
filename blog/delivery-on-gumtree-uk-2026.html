Delivery on Gumtree UK (2026): Fees, Buyer Protection, Evri Labels and Pay & Collect

13 November 2026 Selling 8 min read

Gumtree is no longer only for cash on collection. With Delivery on Gumtree, a buyer anywhere in the UK can press Buy Now, pay online and get the item sent by Evri. With Pay & Collect, a local buyer pays online before they collect. Gumtree says there are no seller fees. The buyer pays the delivery cost and a Buyer Protection fee.

Every Gumtree figure on this page comes from Gumtree’s own Help Centre and announcements, checked on 13 November 2026. Gumtree’s pages are not always consistent with each other; where they differ, this page says so.

What changed

  • 12 November 2025: Gumtree named Evri as its first official delivery partner. Gumtree said this was “the first time Gumtree will allow buyers to pay for items directly on the site”. Delivery was to start in selected categories such as fashion, accessories and baby & children, with “a wider launch planned for 2026”.
  • April 2026: Gumtree added pick-up points. Buyers can choose delivery to a shop or a locker instead of home.
  • September 2026: Gumtree started to roll out online payment for collection, called Pay & Collect, for For Sale listings under £250.

Sources: Gumtree press release, 12 November 2025; Gumtree, Introducing Pick-up Points (15 April 2026); Gumtree, online payment guide (16 September 2026)

Gumtree does not publish one fixed percentage. Its Buyer Protection Policy gives the range of 5% to 10%. One part of an older Gumtree article, from November 2025, shows “£0.70 +5%”. The fee shows on the ad and at checkout. Gumtree’s Help Centre pages give no delivery prices.

You keep the full item price. Your own costs are the item, the packaging and your time. If you bought the T-shirt for £3 at a car boot sale and used a £0.50 mailing bag, your profit is £20 − £3 − £0.50 = £16.50.

Who and what can use delivery

  • Private sellers only. Gumtree’s terms say the services “are available for eligible Ads provided by private sellers only”. That section does not define a private seller. If you trade as a business, check with Gumtree before you use delivery.
  • UK only. You must be a UK resident, and the item must be in your possession and in the UK. Buyers need a UK delivery address. Card payments need a UK-registered card, and payments are in GBP.
  • £250 limit. Only items priced up to £250 are eligible. You can still list an item above £250, but without online payment. Gumtree’s terms say the price limit “will vary by category”.
  • Up to 5 kg. The parcel must weigh no more than 5 kg. Length + 2 × width + 2 × height must be under 245 cm, and no side can be longer than 120 cm.
  • Selected categories. Gumtree’s Help Centre list (30 October 2025) says only these items are eligible: clothing, footwear and accessories for women, men and kids; occasion and bridal wear; linen and bedding; kids’ toys; music, films, books and games (not vinyl); and sporting goods. A June 2026 Gumtree article also lists tech and gaming, headphones and speakers, and small appliances.
  • Exclusions. Large or bulky items are not eligible. Gumtree’s November 2025 guide also names watches, high-value jewellery and vinyl.

Gumtree says it adds categories over time. When you post an ad, the delivery switch appears only if your category is eligible.

Sources: Gumtree, Eligible items for delivery; Gumtree, Eligibility to use payments; Gumtree, Choosing parcel size; Gumtree, How delivery works for sellers (30 June 2026); Gumtree, Delivery on Gumtree: everything you need to know (20 November 2025); Gumtree terms of use

How to sell with delivery, step by step

  1. Post your ad and check the delivery switch. Gumtree’s Help Centre (October 2025) says the switch is on by default for eligible items. Gumtree’s 2026 articles tell you to switch it on. Check it on each ad. You can turn delivery off at any time before the item sells.
  2. Pick the parcel size. Light is up to 1 kg (a T-shirt, a paperback). Medium is up to 2 kg (jeans, trainers). Heavy or multiple items is up to 5 kg (winter boots, a clothing bundle). If the parcel is bigger or heavier than the size you chose, additional charges may apply.
  3. Add your bank details. Gumtree pays through Mangopay. To receive payouts, you complete an identity check.
  4. When it sells, get the label. Gumtree gives you a prepaid Evri label. Print it at home, or show the QR code at the drop-off point. Do not change the label.
  5. Send it within 7 calendar days. Drop it at an Evri ParcelShop or locker. If you don’t send it in time, Gumtree cancels the order and refunds the buyer. Delivery can take up to 4 working days after drop-off.
  6. Get paid. Gumtree releases your money when the buyer confirms they are happy, or 48 hours after the carrier confirms delivery if there is no dispute.

Sources: Gumtree, Not wanting to use delivery; Gumtree, Packaging and delivery time; Gumtree, Getting paid for items you’ve sold; Gumtree, You asked, we delivered

What the buyer gets

  • Delivery to home, or to an Evri shop or locker. A locker usually holds the parcel for 3 calendar days and a shop for 7 days (a newer Gumtree article says 7–10 days). If the buyer does not collect it, the item may go back to the seller.
  • Payment by Visa or Mastercard, Apple Pay or PayPal. Mangopay holds the money until the order completes.
  • Buyer Protection if the item does not arrive, arrives damaged, or is “significantly not as described”. The buyer must report it in My Orders within 48 hours of confirmed delivery.

Buyer Protection does not cover a change of mind, or small flaws that the listing described or showed. So describe and photograph every flaw.

Sources: Gumtree, How delivery on Gumtree works; Gumtree, How payments work with online payments (16 September 2026); Gumtree, Buyer Protection Policy

Seller protection

Gumtree’s November 2025 guide lists “Compensation up to £250 for lost or damaged items if guidelines are followed” and support for ‘significantly not as described’ disputes. Gumtree’s Help Centre lists the seller’s duties: choose the right parcel size, pack the item securely, use the label without changes and send within 7 days.

While Gumtree checks a buyer’s complaint, it can put your payment on hold.

Sources: Gumtree, Delivery on Gumtree: everything you need to know (20 November 2025); Gumtree, Payments on hold

Pay & Collect: online payment for local pickup

Pay & Collect is for items that the buyer collects in person. It works for For Sale listings under £250:

  1. The buyer presses Buy Now and pays online. The item is reserved for them.
  2. You agree a time and place in Gumtree messages.
  3. The buyer checks the item when they collect it.
  4. If they are happy, they give you a 6-digit collection code.
  5. You enter the code in My Orders > Sold > View Order Details. Gumtree releases the money to you at once. Your bank may take longer to show it.

After you enter the code, the sale is complete and can’t be reversed. Gumtree says the transaction is then “not eligible for disputes or claims”. If the buyer is not happy at collection, they cancel the order and get a refund.

Gumtree’s guide is not consistent about ads that are already live. One part says eligible listings become available automatically. Its FAQ says you need to edit the ad and switch on online payment. Check your ad. Gumtree does not say whether the buyer pays a Buyer Protection fee on Pay & Collect.

Sources: Gumtree, How Pay & Collect works (30 September 2026); Gumtree, online payment guide

Our view: should you switch delivery on?

  • Yes, for small items that sell nationally: clothes, books, games and toys. You reach buyers outside your area, and you pay no fee.
  • For local buyers who want to see the item first, Pay & Collect lets them check it before you are paid.
  • No, for bulky items or items over £250. They are not eligible.

The buyer sees the Buyer Protection fee and the delivery cost on top of your price. Compare your total with similar items on Vinted and eBay before you set the price. Our guides to Vinted fees and eBay fees for UK private sellers explain what buyers pay there.

Tax

If you only sell your own unwanted things, this is usually not trading. If you buy items to resell at a profit, the income can be taxable. The rules are the same on every site. See the £1,000 trading allowance and the rise to £3,000 from April 2027.

FAQ

Does Gumtree charge sellers a fee for delivery?

No. Gumtree says there are no seller fees or costs when you use payments on Gumtree. The buyer pays the delivery cost and a Buyer Protection fee. Optional ad promotions still cost money.

How much is the Gumtree Buyer Protection fee?

£0.70 plus 5% to 10% of the item price, depending on the item’s value and category. On a £20 item, that is between £1.70 and £2.70. The buyer pays it.

Which courier does Gumtree use?

Evri. Gumtree says Evri is currently its only delivery partner. You get a prepaid Evri label or a QR code after the sale.

How long do I have to send the item?

7 calendar days after the buyer pays. If you don’t send it in time, Gumtree cancels the order and refunds the buyer.

When do I get paid?

When the buyer confirms they are happy, or 48 hours after the carrier confirms delivery if no dispute is raised. With Pay & Collect, you are paid when you enter the buyer’s 6-digit collection code.

Can I turn delivery off?

Yes. Gumtree says you can turn delivery off at any time before the item sells. Check the delivery switch when you post the ad, because Gumtree’s pages disagree on whether it is on by default.
