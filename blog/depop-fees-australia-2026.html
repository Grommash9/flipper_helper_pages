Depop Fees Australia 2026: No Selling Fee Since 22 July — What Sellers Pay Now

14 June 2026 Fees 7 min read

Since 22 July 2026, Depop charges no selling fee to sellers based in Australia on AUD sales. You still pay a payment processing fee of 2.6% + $0.30 AUD. On a $50 sale where the buyer pays $10 shipping, that is $1.86. Buyers now pay a marketplace fee of up to 5% + up to $1 on top of the item price.

Update, 12 November 2026: an earlier version of this page said Australian sellers still pay a 10% selling fee. That stopped on 22 July 2026. Every figure below was checked against Depop, eBay, Vinted and ATO pages on 12 November 2026.

Depop’s fee page says: “No Depop Selling fees will be charged to sellers based in the UK, US, or AUS.” If a sale is refunded, Depop reverses the seller fees too.

Shipping: Depop Help says “Depop Shipping is the only option for new listings, for both new and existing users. Existing listings will stay as ‘Other’ and won’t be affected.” With Depop Shipping you get a prepaid Australia Post label. Depop also says the service “is currently being rolled out in phases”, so check what your account shows (Depop Help, How to ship – Australia).

The examples on this page assume no taxes on the sale. If taxes apply, the processing fee is charged on them too. For an older listing that you ship manually, you also pay for postage yourself.

Example: $20 sale, buyer pays $10 shipping

  • $20 item + $10 shipping = $30 gross
  • Payment processing: 2.6% of $30 + $0.30 = $1.08
  • Total Depop fees: $1.08, about 3.6% of $30.

The fixed $0.30 matters more on small sales, but the total is far smaller than under the old selling fee.

Example: the same $50 sale, boosted

  • With a Depop Shipping label (the only option for new listings), the boosting fee is charged on the item price: 8% of $50 = $4.00.
  • Payment processing: $1.86
  • Total Depop fees: $4.00 + $1.86 = $5.86

For an older listing shipped manually, the boosting fee covers shipping too: 8% of ($50 + $10) = $4.80, so the total is $6.66.

What the buyer pays: the marketplace fee

Since 22 July 2026, Australian buyers who pay in AUD pay a marketplace fee of “up to 5% of the item purchase price (excluding taxes and shipping costs), plus a fixed amount of up to A$1” (Depop Help).

  • On a $50 item, the most it can be is 5% of $50 + $1 = $3.50. The exact amount shows at checkout.
  • On a bundle, the percentage is charged on the combined price and the fixed part once per transaction.
  • It is refunded in full if the item is refunded.
  • Depop’s Terms of Service say: “In Australia, the Marketplace Fee is inclusive of GST.”

The buyer fee does not come out of your payout. But buyers see the total at checkout, so it can affect what they are willing to offer.

What changed on 22 July 2026

Depop announced the change on 3 July 2026: “Selling fees will be removed for sellers based in Australia on AUD sales from 22 July 2026. Payment processing fees and boosting fees will continue to apply.” The buyer marketplace fee started on the same day (Depop newsroom).

Depop says the move “broadly aligns Australia with Depop’s fee structure in the UK and US”. Sellers outside the UK, US and Australia who sell in a currency other than GBP, USD or AUD still pay a Depop selling fee of 10% on all sales.

eBay now owns Depop

eBay agreed to buy Depop from Etsy on 18 February 2026 “for approximately $1.2 billion in cash, subject to certain purchase price adjustments”, in US dollars (joint announcement). eBay completed the purchase on 30 July 2026 (eBay press release).

Depop announced the Australian fee change on 3 July 2026, before the purchase closed.

What the buyer pays on a $50 item:

  • Depop: up to $3.50 (5% of $50 + $1).
  • eBay Australia: $0.30 + 8% of $20 + 6% of $30 = $3.70.
  • Vinted Australia: usually 5% of $50 + $1.20 = $3.70.

Things to know about eBay Australia’s free selling:

  • The $25,000 is an eligibility rule, not an allowance. eBay counts item price, postage and tax over the past 12 months. Go over it and eBay moves you to a Pro Starter plan from the 1st of the next month, and final value fees apply.
  • Labels. eBay says that as part of free selling “you’ll generally have to use the prepaid shipping label provided by eBay or buy labels on eBay to send your eligible items.” eBay lists the exceptions on its prepaid label help page.

Vinted launched in Australia on 1 July 2026. Fees are only one part of the choice: each platform has a different audience. Many resellers list on more than one platform; our cross-listing guide covers how to avoid selling the same item twice.

How to work out your actual profit per item

Real profit is the sale price minus the item cost, all fees, any postage you pay yourself, and packaging. An example: you buy a vintage band tee at an op shop for $4 and sell it on Depop for $40. The buyer pays $10 shipping, and you send it with the Depop Shipping label.

  • Item price: $40
  • Depop selling fee: $0
  • Payment processing: 2.6% of ($40 + $10) + $0.30 = $1.60
  • Packaging: say $1
  • Item cost: $4
  • Profit: $40 − $1.60 − $1 − $4 = $33.40

This assumes the buyer’s shipping payment covers the label. For an older listing that you ship manually, subtract the postage you paid too. On small items, postage can eat most of the profit.

Tax: do you need to declare Depop sales?

The ATO separates a business, an income-producing activity that is not a business, and casual sales of unwanted personal items. It says: “It is important to remember that ‘not being in business’ does not automatically mean amounts you receive are tax-free.” (ATO, online selling)

The ATO’s own example is a person who buys items to resell at a profit and is not carrying on a business. Even so, “the amounts earned are assessable income that must be declared in their tax return.” Selling your own unwanted items is usually not assessable income.

If you are in business, you must register for GST when your GST turnover reaches $75,000 (ATO, registering for GST). This is not tax advice. Speak to a registered tax agent if you are unsure.

FAQ

How much does Depop take from Australian sellers in 2026?

Since 22 July 2026, no selling fee on AUD sales. You pay 2.6% + $0.30 AUD payment processing on the item price plus shipping and any taxes. On a $50 sale where the buyer pays $10 shipping, that is $1.86. Boosting costs an extra 8% and is optional.

What is the simplest way to estimate Depop fees before listing?

Multiply the total the buyer pays you (item + shipping) by 0.026 and add $0.30. For $60, that is $1.56 + $0.30 = $1.86. Add 8% if you boost.

Who pays the Depop marketplace fee?

The buyer. It is up to 5% of the item price plus up to $1, and it does not come out of your payout.

Does the Depop fee include GST?

Depop’s Terms of Service say the marketplace fee is inclusive of GST in Australia. Depop’s pages don’t say whether the 2.6% + $0.30 processing fee includes GST. If you are GST-registered, ask your tax agent.

Is Vinted available in Australia?

Yes, since 1 July 2026. Sellers pay no selling fee, and buyers pay a Vinted fee that is usually 5% + $1.20.
