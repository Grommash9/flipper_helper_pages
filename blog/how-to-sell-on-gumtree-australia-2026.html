How to Sell on Gumtree Australia (2026): Fees, Gumtree Pay & Shipping

14 June 2026 Selling 10 min read

Most Gumtree Australia categories are free to list, and Gumtree Pay is free for sellers. Buyers who pay through Gumtree Pay pay a 3% platform fee. Sellers can now ship items through the Shipping feature in Gumtree Pay, with tracking and Protected Shipping for eligible sales of $1,000 or less.

Update, 13 November 2026: an earlier version of this page said that cash on pickup is the only safe payment, and it described a paid “Made it” delivery service. Gumtree now offers Gumtree Pay for pickup and shipped sales, and we found no “Made it” service on Gumtree’s pages. Every fee and rule below was checked against Gumtree Australia help pages, eBay Australia, Depop and the ATO on 13 November 2026.

Gumtree Pay: what it costs

Gumtree Pay is Gumtree’s own payment system. Payments go through Adyen. Buyers can pay with cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayTo. You can use it for pickup sales and for shipped sales.

WhoFee
SellerNone. You get the full amount you agreed with the buyer, including the shipping cost.
Buyer, most payment methods3% of the item price plus shipping. Minimum $1, maximum $200. Includes GST.
Buyer, PayTo0.3% (a 90% discount). The $1 minimum still applies.

Examples of the buyer’s fee:

  • $20 item, pickup: 3% is $0.60, so the buyer pays the $1 minimum.
  • $100 item + $15 shipping: 3% of $115 = $3.45.
  • $10,000 vehicle: 3% would be $300, so the buyer pays the $200 maximum (Gumtree’s own example).

Your payout goes to your bank account and usually arrives in 3–5 business days. Before you can get paid, you must complete identity and bank verification (KYC). Gumtree recommends that you do this before your first sale.

Sources: Gumtree AU, Gumtree Pay Service Fees; Gumtree AU, Gumtree Pay FAQ

Shipping with Gumtree Pay

Gumtree is not the carrier. You choose the delivery provider and send the item yourself, but you set up the shipping inside Gumtree Pay. How it works:

  1. Tick “Shipping” when you post your ad. Buyers then see a “Shipping Offered” badge.
  2. When a buyer asks you to ship, agree the shipping cost in the chat.
  3. Create the payment request in the chat. Select Shipping, enter the shipping cost and select a delivery provider from the approved list. The buyer sees the shipping cost as a separate line at checkout.
  4. When the buyer pays, pack the item and send it with the provider you selected.
  5. Enter a valid tracking number within 3 days of payment. If you don’t, the order may be cancelled and the buyer refunded.
  6. When the provider marks the item as delivered, the buyer has 3 days to inspect it. You get paid when the buyer confirms, or when the 3 days end without a dispute.

Tracking works through AfterShip. The approved providers include Australia Post and DHL. You see the full list when you create a payment request. Make an Offer does not work with shipping yet: if you accept an offer and the buyer then asks for shipping, create a separate payment request.

Categories that offer shipping: Clothing & Jewellery; Baby & Children; Books, Music & Games; Antiques, Art & Collectables; Electronics & Computer; Sport & Fitness. Art and collectables can be shipped, but Protected Shipping does not cover them.

How to work out the shipping cost

  • Pack the item first. Then measure and weigh the parcel.
  • Most providers charge on the actual weight or the volumetric weight, whichever is greater. Gumtree gives the formula: length × width × height (cm) ÷ 5,000.
  • Get a quote from the provider’s website, or use a flat-rate service if you don’t know the buyer’s location yet.

Sources: Gumtree AU, Seller Shipping Guide; Gumtree AU, Shipping with Gumtree Pay FAQ

Protected Shipping: what it covers for sellers

Gumtree Pay with Protected Shipping may apply when the buyer pays with Gumtree Pay and you ship with a provider selected in the Shipping feature. It covers items of $1,000 or less. The seller policy has an effective date of June 2026. Gumtree says the cover “is not an insurance policy, a warranty, or a guarantee”, and Gumtree decides each case.

  • Funds are held. Gumtree holds the buyer’s money until the buyer confirms the item, or until the 3-day inspection window ends.
  • False claims. If a buyer says the item is significantly not as described, and your photos and listing show the claim is false, Gumtree releases the payout to you.
  • Wrong address. If the buyer gave the wrong address and you shipped with valid tracking to that address, Gumtree does not hold back your payout for that reason.
  • Chargebacks for non-delivery. Gumtree gives proof of fulfilment to help you dispute them.

What it does not cover:

  • Loss or damage in transit. You are responsible for the item until the buyer confirms it. Gumtree “strongly recommend[s]” shipping insurance for the full value.
  • Shipping outside the feature. If you use another provider, or a supported provider outside the feature, you and the buyer lose all protection. This also applies when the buyer chose ‘Pickup’ at checkout and you then post the item.
  • Payments outside Gumtree Pay, such as direct PayPal, PayID or bank transfer.
  • Some item types, for example custom-made goods, art and collectables, perishables, digital goods, hygiene-sensitive items and hazardous materials.

Returns. If an item is significantly not as described, Gumtree “may require you to pay for, or reimburse the buyer for, the cost of return shipping”. When the returned item arrives, you have 3 days to confirm receipt or report a problem with its condition. If you do nothing, Gumtree treats this as confirmation and refunds the buyer.

Your listing must have at least one clear photo of the item. Gumtree uses your photos as the main evidence in a dispute. Without photos, your protection can be reduced or removed.

Source: Gumtree AU, Protected Shipping Policy for Sellers

What to sell on Gumtree Australia

Ideas for items that buyers usually collect in person:

  • Furniture — IKEA, mid-century pieces, sofas, dining tables. Check recent listings in your suburb before you set a price.
  • Whitegoods — fridges, washing machines, dryers. Describe the condition honestly. Buyers often test before they pay.
  • Baby and kids’ gear — prams, cots, high chairs. For car seats, check the manufacturer’s expiry date.
  • Tools and garden equipment — list seasonal items before the season starts.
  • Bicycles and exercise equipment.

With shipping in Gumtree Pay, smaller items in the shipping categories (clothing, books, electronics, sport) can also reach buyers in other states.

Gumtree listing tips

  1. Photos. Use natural light and a plain background. Show the item from several angles and show every flaw. For shipped sales, the photos are your evidence in a dispute.
  2. Title. Brand + item + size or model + condition. Example: IKEA Kallax 4×4 White Bookcase, Excellent Condition. Do not use all-caps or “must go!”.
  3. Category. Pick the most specific subcategory.
  4. Description. Give the dimensions and weight so that buyers can plan transport. List what is included and any flaws.
  5. Price. Compare with similar listings near you. Leave some room if you expect to negotiate.
  6. Shipping. If the item is in a shipping category and you can post it, tick Shipping. If you know the postage cost, put it in the description.
  7. Contact. Keep all chats in Gumtree messages.

How to avoid Gumtree scams

Gumtree’s help centre warns: “Keep all chats within Gumtree Messenger. Scammers may ask to move to WhatsApp or SMS and send fake payment links. Gumtree never arranges payments or deliveries outside our platform.”

  • Fake “Gumtree delivery” messages. Gumtree says: if you get a WhatsApp or SMS message from a buyer that offers Gumtree delivery as a service, do not click the link or enter your payment details.
  • Bank transfer. Gumtree does not recommend payment by bank transfer. Payments outside Gumtree Pay, such as direct PayPal, PayID or bank transfer, are not protected.
  • Shipping outside Gumtree Pay. If you ship, use Gumtree Pay with a provider from the Shipping feature. Otherwise you have no Protected Shipping cover.
  • Pickup sales. For pickup, Gumtree recommends that the buyer inspects the item before paying, because Protected Shipping does not apply.

Sources: Gumtree AU, Seller Shipping Guide; Gumtree AU, Gumtree Pay FAQ

Lowballer replies

  • “Price is firm, thanks.” Ends the negotiation politely.
  • “Closest I’ll do is $X.” Give one number and stop.
  • “First in, first served. Message me when you’re ready to pick up.” Shows that you are not holding the item.
  • No reply. You do not have to answer every offer.

Keep replies short. A long explanation of your price usually starts a longer haggle. For more replies, see How to Deal with Lowballers.

Tax: do you need to declare Gumtree sales?

The ATO separates a business, an income-producing activity that is not a business, and casual sales of unwanted personal items. It says: “It is important to remember that ‘not being in business’ does not automatically mean amounts you receive are tax-free.” (ATO, online selling)

The ATO’s own example is a person who buys items to resell at a profit and is not carrying on a business. Even so, “the amounts earned are assessable income that must be declared in their tax return.” Selling your own unwanted items is usually not assessable income.

If you are in business, you must register for GST when your GST turnover reaches $75,000 (ATO, registering for GST). This is not tax advice. Speak to a registered tax agent if you are unsure.

FAQ

Is it free to sell on Gumtree Australia?

In most categories, yes. Listing fees apply to some vehicle categories, pets and animals, and to extra ads in a few categories such as Real Estate. Gumtree Pay is free for sellers. Paid features such as Top Ad are optional.

Does Gumtree Australia offer shipping?

Yes, through the Shipping feature in Gumtree Pay. You enter the shipping cost and select an approved delivery provider in the payment request, send the item yourself and add tracking within 3 days. Gumtree is not the carrier.

How much is the Gumtree Pay fee?

The buyer pays 3% of the item price plus shipping, with a $1 minimum and a $200 maximum, including GST. With PayTo it is 0.3%, with the same $1 minimum. Sellers pay nothing.

When do I get paid for a shipped Gumtree sale?

When the buyer confirms the item, or when the 3-day inspection window after delivery ends with no dispute. The money usually reaches your bank in 3–5 business days. You must complete identity and bank verification first.

How does Gumtree compare to eBay Australia?

Neither charges an eligible seller a selling fee. Both add a fee for the buyer: on a $50 item with no shipping, $1.50 on Gumtree Pay and $3.70 on eBay. eBay free selling needs an Australian registration address, no Pro plan and up to $25,000 in sales in 12 months, and you generally must use eBay labels. Gumtree lets you choose an approved provider in Gumtree Pay.

Do I pay tax on Gumtree sales in Australia?

The casual sale of your own unwanted items is usually not assessable income. In the ATO’s example, a person who buys items to resell at a profit is not in business, but the amounts are still assessable income. Speak to a registered tax agent if you are unsure.
