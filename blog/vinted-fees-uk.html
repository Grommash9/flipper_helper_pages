Vinted Fees UK Explained: What Sellers and Buyers Pay (2026)

17 April 2026 Guides 6 min read

The short answer: Vinted charges no selling fees. There is no listing fee and no commission. You list a jumper for £15, someone buys it, you get £15.

The buyer pays a Vinted fee on top of the item price, plus shipping. Until October 2026, this fee was called the Buyer Protection fee. This guide explains how each cost works. If you’re new to Vinted, see our complete guide to selling on Vinted UK first.

Seller fees: £0

Vinted’s help centre says: “There are no fees for selling on Vinted, and you can list as many items as you like.” There is no listing fee, no final value fee and no commission.

You set your price, and the buyer pays that price. After the order completes, the money goes to your Vinted Balance within 2 days. Vinted’s price list has no fee for a withdrawal to your bank account. A withdrawal can take up to 5 business days to arrive.

Compare this with eBay business sellers. eBay charges them a final value fee when an item sells. For most clothing, the fee is 11.9% of the total sale amount, including postage, plus 30p or 40p per order. eBay also charges a 0.35% regulatory operating fee. These eBay fees exclude VAT.

For example, take a £5 clothing order with free postage. eBay’s fees on that sale for a business seller are about 91p before VAT (11.9% + 0.35% of £5, plus 30p). Listing fees or an eBay Shop subscription can come on top. On Vinted, the same £5 sale pays you £5.

Sellers can choose to pay for two optional services: Bumps and Showcase. The section “Do sellers pay for anything?” below explains them.

Sources: Vinted Help, Selling basics; Vinted Price List; Vinted Help, Withdraw your earnings; eBay UK, Fees for business sellers

The Vinted fee (formerly Buyer Protection): usually 3–8% + £0.30–£0.80

Every order made with the “Buy now” button includes a fee that the buyer pays. Vinted does not take it from the seller. Vinted’s UK terms and price list, both dated 8 October 2026, call it the Vinted fee. Before that, Vinted called it the Buyer Protection fee. According to Vinted’s official price list, the fee is usually:

  • 3% to 8% of the item or bundle price
  • Plus a fixed fee of £0.30 to £0.80

The exact amount depends on the item, the order value, and whether it’s a single item or a bundle. Prices include VAT. Vinted shows the fee in each listing’s price breakdown and at checkout, before the buyer pays.

What changed in October 2026: Vinted now calls the fee “a mandatory platform fee”. Vinted says the fee helps cover the operational and development costs of the platform. Before that, Vinted’s price list described it as a fee for its Buyer Protection services. Vinted’s terms set out the refunds for items that are lost, damaged or significantly not as described. If you’re in a dispute, check them.

The buyer can get the Vinted fee back in some refunds. If a buyer gets a refund for a single item under Vinted’s refund policy, the terms say the Vinted fee is refunded in full. Some refunds cover only the significantly-not-as-described items in a bundle. Then Vinted recalculates the fee and refunds the difference.

Sources: Vinted Price List; Vinted Help, Vinted fee; Vinted Terms and Conditions; earlier Vinted Price List with the Buyer Protection fee (archived 9 September 2026)

Shipping costs: paid by the buyer

The buyer also pays for shipping. When you list an item, you choose a parcel size. At checkout, the buyer chooses a shipping option and buys a prepaid shipping label for you.

Vinted’s shipping help page lists these shipping providers: Evri, InPost, DPD, Royal Mail, Relay, AnyVan and DHL Express. Vinted’s terms say the price depends on the parcel size, shipping route, carrier and any discounts. The buyer sees the shipping cost at checkout. See Vinted’s shipping page for the current options.

You (the seller) don’t pay for this postage. Vinted sends you a digital shipping label or code. You must use that label to send the order. If you use a different label or the wrong shipping option, Vinted cancels the order automatically.

Sources: Vinted Help, Shipping on Vinted; Vinted Help, Uploading step by step; Vinted Terms and Conditions

Worked example: what does a £15 sale look like?

For example, you list a branded jumper for £15 on Vinted.

What the seller gets

  • You receive: £15.00 (your full listed price)
  • Vinted deducts: £0.00
  • Your payout: £15.00

What the buyer pays

  • Item price: £15.00
  • Vinted fee (example rate of 5% + £0.70, inside Vinted’s usual range): £1.45
  • Shipping: shown at checkout (it depends on the carrier and parcel size)
  • Buyer’s total: £16.45 + shipping

The seller gets £15. The buyer pays £16.45 plus shipping. Vinted keeps the £1.45 fee. The shipping cost pays for the label.

The right platform depends on what you sell. For non-fashion items, see our guides to selling on eBay and Facebook Marketplace.

Do sellers pay for anything?

In normal use, no. But there are two optional costs:

  • Bumps (promoted listings): A Bump moves one listing higher in newsfeeds and search results for 3 or 7 days, or until it sells. The price depends on the item price and the Bump duration
  • Showcase (formerly Wardrobe Spotlight): Showcase promotes all your listings, not one item. It lasts 7 days, and you need at least 5 items listed. The price depends on how many listings you have and their value

Neither of these is required. You can sell on Vinted without them.

What about returns and disputes? A buyer can ask for a refund if an item is lost, damaged or significantly not as described. If you or the buyer report the issue to Vinted, Vinted’s Customer Support makes the final decision. If the buyer must send the item back, the terms say the buyer pays the return shipping, unless you agree otherwise. Honest, accurate listings with good photos help you avoid disputes. To see how Vinted handles disputes, read Vinted’s terms and conditions.

Sources: Vinted Price List; Vinted Help, Item Bumps; Vinted Help, Showcase; Vinted Terms and Conditions

Vinted Pro: when you sell as a business

This is the short version. Our full Vinted Pro guide gives the details on sign-up, returns, HMRC and platform reporting.

Vinted’s terms say a standard account is for personal use, not for professional purposes. If you sell as a professional, Vinted says you need a Vinted Pro account. From Vinted’s Pro page: “If you want to sell a high volume of items on Vinted (more than just clearing out your closet!), you need to sign up as a professional seller.”

Conditions (checked November 2026)

  • Who can join: you must be registered as a business. Sole traders, non-profit organisations, and companies are eligible.
  • Sign-up: you give your registration details, such as a company registration number, so Vinted can confirm your business.
  • Cost: free. Vinted says Pro is “entirely free” and you can list as many second-hand items as you like.
  • Two accounts: you can keep a standard account and a separate Pro account. Each account needs a different email address and phone number.
  • Returns: you must accept the Vinted Pro terms of use and terms of sale. These terms let the buyer return an item within 14 days of receiving it. You can also add your own return and refund policies.
  • Public details: your company name, company number, contact details, trade registrar and business address show on your profile. Vinted’s Pro FAQ says sole traders can keep these private. But its Pro guide says buyers must still get your name and business address. See our Vinted Pro guide.
  • What you can sell:
    • clothing, footwear and accessories
    • children’s toys, furniture and childcare equipment
    • tech accessories
    • homeware
    • entertainment (books, consoles and games, board games, music, video)
    • pet care items

What this means for resellers

Pro doesn’t add a seller fee, so your margin per sale stays the same. The real costs are the 14-day returns and the public display of your business details. Include the cost of returns in your prices. The category list also does not include some common reseller categories, such as antiques and collectables. Check the list before you move stock to your Pro account.

Vinted’s Pro page and Pro guide don’t say how many sales make you “high volume”. GOV.UK says you’re probably trading if you sell goods that you “bought intending to sell for a profit”. If your total trading income is more than the £1,000 trading allowance for the tax year, GOV.UK says you must tell HMRC. Vinted’s Pro guide says Vinted can ask you to create a Pro account. Vinted can also block your access if you meet the criteria for a professional and don’t register.

Sources: Vinted Pro; Vinted Pro guide; Vinted Terms and Conditions; GOV.UK, Check if you need to tell HMRC about income from online platforms

Frequently asked questions

Does Vinted charge sellers fees?

No. Vinted’s help centre says there are no fees for selling on Vinted. You get the full item price. The buyer pays a Vinted fee (usually 3–8% + £0.30–£0.80) on top of the item price. Bumps and Showcase are optional paid extras for sellers.

What is the Vinted fee (Buyer Protection fee)?

It is a mandatory platform fee that the buyer pays at checkout. It is usually 3–8% of the item or bundle price plus £0.30–£0.80. Vinted’s terms and price list dated 8 October 2026 call it the Vinted fee. Before that, Vinted called it the Buyer Protection fee.

Who pays for shipping on Vinted?

The buyer. At checkout, the buyer chooses a shipping option and pays for a prepaid shipping label. Vinted then sends the seller a digital shipping label or code.

How does Vinted compare to eBay fees?

Vinted sellers pay no selling fees. eBay says it is free for UK private sellers to list and sell, with 300 free listings a month. eBay business sellers pay a final value fee (11.9% for most clothing), 30p or 40p per order and a 0.35% regulatory operating fee. These eBay fees exclude VAT.

What is Vinted Pro and do I need it?

Vinted Pro is a free account for business sellers. If you sell a high volume of items as a business, Vinted says you need it. Sole traders, non-profits and companies can join. Buyers can return Pro items within 14 days, and your business details show on your profile.
