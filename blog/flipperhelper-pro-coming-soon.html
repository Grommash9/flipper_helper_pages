FlipperHelper Pro Is Coming Soon — and the Free App Stays Free

16 November 2026 Product 8 min read

Update, 22 November 2026: Pro is launching, and teams are in the first version after all. Read what is in Pro and how it works.

FlipperHelper Pro is nearly ready. I have worked on it for several months, in my free time. I do not have much free time, so it took longer than I wanted.

This post explains what Pro does, why it costs money when the app does not, and how it works under the hood. The last part is for the technical readers, but I tried to keep it in plain words.

The free app stays free

I built FlipperHelper for my wife, Valentina, who resells. I wanted her to have a really good app that works offline at markets and costs nothing. That has not changed, and it will not change:

  • Everything a person needs to start reselling from zero stays free.
  • I will never remove a feature that is already in the free app.
  • There are no ads, and I do not plan to add them.

Android users often write in their reviews that they like that the app is free and has no ads. It means a lot to me that people notice this. Yes, I could add ads and earn something from them. But I want to make a really good product, I am not a greedy person, and I am fine with earning a bit less.

The free app can stay free because of how it is built. In the free app, your items and photos live on your phone, not on our servers, and exports go to your own Google Drive and Google Sheets. I do pay for the app stores and for this website. But those costs stay small, even when more people use the app.

Why Pro is a subscription

The main Pro feature is the eBay connection. For it to work, eBay must send a message every time you list or sell something, at any hour of the day. Something has to receive those messages and process them. That needs servers, and servers cost money.

Unlike the free app, this cost grows with use. More people, more listings and more orders need more capacity. I cannot run that at a loss forever, so the features that need servers are part of a paid subscription.

eBay sales record themselves

This feature came from a real problem at home. When something sells on eBay, the costs of that sale are often not final yet. To see what you really earned, you have to come back a few hours later, sometimes the next day, and look through the transaction history. Only then can you record the sale.

So Valentina often did not record her eBay sales at all. The app then showed less than the real numbers. And you can only trust an app that has all of them.

With Pro, you connect your eBay account once. Then:

  • When you list an item on eBay, FlipperHelper marks it as listed.
  • When the item sells, FlipperHelper records the sale.
  • The costs of the sale, such as eBay fees, promoted listing (ad) fees and the postage label, are added to the item. eBay does not show them all at once, so FlipperHelper checks for them 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours and 72 hours after the sale.

To match an eBay listing to your item, FlipperHelper uses the item's SKU, the code that starts with FH-. Put it in eBay's Custom label field, or in the title or the description. If a listing or a sale cannot be matched, it waits for you on the website, and you link it by hand.

Your items on every device, and on the web

A while ago, someone emailed me to ask if they could add items from a computer. A web version was never part of the plan for FlipperHelper. But I thought: why not?

So Pro has three parts that work together:

  • The iPhone and Android apps. They still work fully offline.
  • Sync. With a Pro account, your changes go to the server, and your other devices download them when they open.
  • A website. There you can see and manage your items: add items, mark them as listed or sold, and look at your hauls and expenses.

You can work the way that suits you. Add items on your phone while you buy stock. Then sit down at the computer to list them, and mark them as listed right there. The person who emailed me buys online, so for them it is easiest to add each item at the desk, right after they buy it.

How sync works

Sync is not live. It runs at set moments, which keeps the server costs down:

  • when you open the app;
  • when you leave the app or it goes to the background;
  • on a timer: every 5 minutes while the app is open on iPhone, and about every 15 minutes on Android, when the system allows it.

So an item you add does not appear on your other devices in the same second. But the app sends your changes every time you leave it.

As before, every change is saved on your phone first, so it takes effect at once. If the signal at a market is weak, you lose nothing. Your change stays on the phone and goes to the server later, when there is a connection.

When two devices change the same thing, the later change wins. Say one phone changes the price you paid for an item at 10:00, and another phone changes it at 11:00. The 11:00 price wins, because whoever wrote last most likely knew the newest facts. “Later” means the time you made the change on the device, not the time it reached the server.

Some changes never clash. If one phone edits an item's title and another phone records its sale, both changes stay. The server also keeps a history of changes, and the website will let you look through it.

With Pro, your data is also stored on the server. The automatic Google Sheets export then turns off, and you export your data from the website instead.

Why not just one server

This is the technical part, and the main reason Pro took so long.

FlipperHelper users live on different continents: in the UK, the US and Australia, and quite a few in Italy. One server in one country would be slow for everyone who lives far from it.

Use also comes in waves. One day someone in Italy shared FlipperHelper on social media. Thank you, if you are reading this! That day the app got many times more downloads than on a normal day. With one server, a wave like that can slow it down, or stop it for everyone at once. I did not want to take that risk. I want the quality to be as high as I can make it.

So the backend runs on three continents at once:

  • North America: AWS region us-east-1, in Northern Virginia.
  • Europe: AWS region eu-central-1, in Frankfurt.
  • Australia and Asia-Pacific: AWS region ap-southeast-2, in Sydney.

Your phone connects to the region that answers it fastest. The regions are linked, and each one copies your data to the other two within seconds. There is also no single machine that a wave can overload. Each request runs as a small function, and AWS starts more copies of it when more requests come in.

I also thought about resellers who travel. Say you live in the US and fly to France to buy stock at the brocantes. At home, your phone uses the US region. In France, it uses Frankfurt. Your data is the same in both places, and the app stays fast in both.

For the technical readers, the short version:

  • Routing: Amazon Route 53 latency-based routing sends each request to the nearest region.
  • Data: Amazon DynamoDB global tables copy the data between the three regions.
  • Compute: AWS Lambda behind Amazon API Gateway, mostly in Go.
  • Conflicts: each group of fields is its own row, and the last writer wins by the time of the change on the device. So a change to one group never overwrites another.

What comes next

Android users have asked for more automation, and I plan to add more of it to Pro. If I can, that includes other marketplaces, so that more of your listings and sales come in by themselves. My plan is to keep one flat price and to add more to it over time.

Pro also lays the base for working as a team. Many resellers start alone, and then the business grows. That is happening for Valentina now: someone else does her listings. In a later update, that person will be able to mark items as listed in FlipperHelper, and Valentina will see it in her own app. Team features are not in the first version of Pro, but the base for them is ready.

That is the idea of the whole app. Start for free. When your business and your needs grow, try Pro. It should make your work simpler.

The first month is free

The first month of Pro will be free, and I think it will stay that way. Seven days is not enough to judge an app like this. You need to find an item, buy it, list it, sell it, and then see the sale and its fees appear by themselves. That takes more than a week.

I am not trying to take your money quickly. My goal is that your business goes well, and that you use FlipperHelper for many years. Next to that, one free month changes nothing.

Quick answers

Will FlipperHelper stay free?

Yes. Everything a person needs to start reselling stays free. No feature that is already in the free app will be removed, and there are no ads.

What does FlipperHelper Pro add?

Yes. Every change is saved on your phone first and takes effect at once. It goes to the server later, when there is a connection.
