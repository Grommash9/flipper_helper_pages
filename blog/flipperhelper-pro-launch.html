FlipperHelper Pro Is Launching: Sync, Teams, eBay and a Web Portal

22 November 2026 Product 11 min read

FlipperHelper Pro is launching. Last week I wrote that it was coming soon. Now the update is with Apple and Google for review. On some stores the review can take a few days, so Pro can reach your phone a little later than this post. But it is close.

This post explains what is in Pro, how each part works, and a few things that are good to know before you start. The free app stays free, and no free feature goes away.

What is in Pro

  • Sync between your devices. Your items, photos and expenses on every phone you sign in on.
  • Teams. Invite one more person to work on your records with you.
  • The eBay connection. Listings, sales and their fees record themselves, and empty item titles fill in from your eBay listings.
  • A web portal at web.flipperhelper.app. Add items and expenses on a computer, see the history of changes, and export your data as CSV.

I want to say this clearly: FlipperHelper is built on your feedback, and it depends on it. The web portal started with one email from a reseller. The eBay connection is what Valentina asked for first, and what many of you asked for too. If something is missing for you, or you want something added, please email me at support@flipperhelper.app. Your requests shape what I build next.

Sync between your devices

With Pro, your changes go to our server, and your other devices download them.

The free app can sync with Google, and that is a good free option when you start. But the Google sync sends all your items every time. When you have thousands of items, each sync takes longer and uses more of your mobile data. Our server takes only what changed. If you have 1,000 items and add one, Google gets 1,001 items, and our server gets one. So sync stays fast and uses little data, however large your stock grows. While Pro is on, the Google sync (photos to Google Drive and the Sheets export) turns off. It comes back if Pro ends.

Sync is not live. The app syncs when you open it, when you leave it, and on a timer: every 5 minutes while the app is open on iPhone, and about every 15 minutes on Android. If you need it now, tap Sync Now in Settings, under the FlipperHelper Pro row.

As before, every change is saved on your phone first, so the app works with no signal at a market. Your changes go to the server when there is a connection.

Teams

With Pro, you can add someone to your team. You both see the same items, hauls and expenses, and you both can add and change them.

I see two main uses. In the first, two people buy stock at the same time, at different stalls or different markets, and each adds the items on their own phone. Later all the items are together, in the order you bought them. Each item's SKU is random and includes the time, so two phones never make the same SKU, and items never overwrite each other. In the second, one person buys, and the other lists and sells.

For now, the limits are:

  • a team has at most 2 people: you and one member;
  • one account can be in at most 5 teams, its own team included.

The limits are there because I do not know the server load yet, and I want to price the plans correctly. More people in a team means more load. If you need a bigger team, email me. A bigger team may become a different plan later.

Only the owner of the team pays. Your team member does not need their own Pro. If the owner's Pro ends, the team stops syncing for both of you, but no data is deleted.

How to invite someone

Team settings are on the web portal only, not in the app. I tried to build only what people asked for, so that I do not have to redo it later.

  1. Open web.flipperhelper.app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Teams, type the person's email under Invite by email, and press Invite.
  3. The other person opens web.flipperhelper.app and signs in with Google or Apple, with the email you invited.
  4. Under Invites to you, they press Accept.
  5. In the app, they choose your team in Settings → Account → Team.

Sign in with Apple can hide your real email behind a relay address. If it does, the web portal shows that address, and the owner invites it.

A member has full access to the records: they can add, change and delete items. A few settings stay with the owner: the home currency, which platforms are on, the team name, invites, and the eBay connection. If enough people ask, I can add access levels later, for example a member who does not see the prices you paid.

The web portal also has a History page. It shows every change in your team, newest first: who made it, on which device, and what changed. With two people in a team, you can always see who did what.

eBay listings and sales record themselves

Valentina asked for the eBay connection first, and so did many of you. The usage numbers say the same: eBay is the platform you list on most. So it came first.

You connect eBay in the app, in Settings → Connect eBay Account. For now, you connect and disconnect eBay only on the phone, not on the web portal.

The eBay page looks bad, and that is OK

When you connect, the app opens eBay's own page. On all my devices it looks quite bad. The preview eBay showed me looked fine, but the real page does not, and its button to agree is hard to see. Sign in to eBay, then find the button that lets FlipperHelper connect, and press it. You then go back to the app. That page comes from eBay, and I cannot change it, sorry. On your phone it may look better.

How FlipperHelper finds your items

FlipperHelper looks for the item's SKU, the code that starts with FH-, in three places on the eBay listing: the Custom label field, the title and the description. When you list from the eBay phone app, there is sometimes no Custom label field. Then put the SKU in the description, and FlipperHelper finds it there.

When a listing matches, the item is marked as listed. When it sells, the item is marked as sold, and the costs of the sale are added to the item as expenses: eBay's fees, other eBay charges for the order such as promoted listing (ad) fees, and the postage label. eBay does not show all of them at once, so FlipperHelper checks again 10 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours and 72 hours after the sale.

This part is important to understand. Say an item sold for £400, and £20 of that went to eBay for advertising. FlipperHelper records a sale for £400 and a £20 eBay expense linked to that sale. So the item brought you £380 before the price you paid for it.

Item titles fill in by themselves

If an item has no title in FlipperHelper, the title of its eBay listing fills it in. A title you wrote yourself never changes.

This helps more than I expected. My wife never types a title. She takes a photo, enters the price, and that is it. When she wanted ChatGPT to look at all her items, it could not tell what each item was. Now eBay has filled in the titles for a large part of her items. Full titles also help with your tax records.

The last 90 days come in too

When you connect, FlipperHelper also loads your eBay history: the listings that ended and the orders of the last 90 days. It does this only if you added at least one item to FlipperHelper in the 30 days before you connect. Your active listings come in at the moment you connect.

Sales that do not match

If a sale or a listing comes from eBay and no SKU matches, it waits on the web portal, on the Unmatched eBay page. There you link it to the right item by hand, even if the eBay listing has no SKU at all.

If something does not update

eBay limits how many requests an app can send each day, and all FlipperHelper users share that limit. I do not know the load yet, so on a busy day we may reach it. Then nothing is lost: the waiting work moves to the next eBay day, which starts at midnight US Pacific time, 8:00 in the UK. So if a sale has not arrived, wait until the next day. If it still has not arrived, disconnect eBay and connect it again. That does not break anything. Or email support.

The web portal

A while ago, a reseller emailed me to ask if they could add items on a computer. They buy online, so it is easier for them to add each item at the desk, right after they buy it. That is how the web portal started, and it will grow the same way, from feedback.

On web.flipperhelper.app, you sign in with Apple or Google. Today you can:

  • add items and expenses, and link expenses to items;
  • add photos, mark items as listed or sold, and cancel a sale;
  • see your dashboard, items, hauls and expenses;
  • link unmatched eBay sales and listings;
  • see the history of changes;
  • manage your team and export your data.

You can see your hauls there, but you create them on the phone. You also delete items on the phone. Everything else works on the phone as before. Tell me which features you need on the web portal, on the phone or on both, and I will add them if I can.

Open the web portal

Export your data as CSV

Before, the app exported your data to Google Sheets. With Pro, you export on the web portal, in Settings → Export. Press Request CSV for your items or for your expenses. The file is made in the background, so you can leave the page. When it is ready, press Download.

Then use the CSV as you like: open it in Google Sheets, send it to your accountant, or give it to ChatGPT.

Each item in the file has a link to its main photo. Anyone with the file can open these links, with no access to your Google Drive. The links work for 30 days after the export. After that, make a new export. So you can share the file with a friend or an accountant, you do not give anyone access to anything, and their access to the photos ends by itself.

Analytics and deleting your account

Usage analytics used to be anonymous. Now, when you are signed in, they are linked to your account, except in the European Economic Area. They are facts such as: the app was opened, an item was added, an item was marked as sold, an item was listed on eBay. There are no amounts, no photos, no item titles or descriptions, and no market or seller names.

I also see how long the server took to answer, for example when you upload a photo or sign in, and the errors you saw. So I can see when the app is slow or broken for you and fix it, even if you never tell me. It also shows me what people use most, and how many of us there are. That is one reason eBay came first.

If you do not want to share this, turn it off. Open Settings, scroll near the end to the About section, and switch off Share usage analytics. The switch is on iPhone and on Android. It does not stop crash and error reports. The privacy policy lists exactly what the app sends.

Deleting your account was also reworked, to meet the store rules and to respect your data. When you delete your account now, we delete your items and photos, your eBay connection, your usage data and the rest of your account, on all your devices. You get an email when it is done. The privacy policy lists the few exceptions. For example, records you added to a team that someone else owns stay with that team.

Moving between iPhone and Android

You can already move your data with a backup file: Export to File on one phone and Import from File on the other. From this update, the iPhone app also reads Android backups. But I know that is not very friendly.

Pro makes it simpler, and the free month is enough for it:

  1. Start the Pro trial on the phone that has your data.
  2. On the new phone, sign in with the same account. The Android app signs in with Google only. If your iPhone uses Sign in with Apple, invite your Google email to your team on the web portal, accept the invite, sign in with Google on Android, and choose the team in Settings → Account → Team.
  3. Wait for the sync. Your items are now on the new phone.

If you then cancel Pro, the data stays on both phones. Only the sync stops. One warning: do not remove the member from the team, and do not delete the old account. That also removes the team's items from the other phone. If you want your Google account to own the team, use Make owner on the Teams page first. The new owner needs their own Pro (a trial is fine) for the team to sync and to connect eBay. Make owner disconnects eBay, so connect it again from the new owner's phone.

The trial helps with eBay in the same way, even if you do not plan to keep Pro. Connect eBay during the trial, and it loads up to 90 days of eBay history and fills in empty titles. If you cancel later, that data stays on your phone. Better data can help your business too: export it and ask ChatGPT how much you make on dresses, T-shirts or side tables.

The first month is free

For new subscribers, the first month of Pro is free, on the monthly and the yearly plan, on iPhone and on Android. After that, you pay the price your app store shows, and you can cancel any time. I want to keep it this way. You should try Pro, see how it works and what it gives you, before you pay anything.

You will not find the price on this site. It depends on your country or region, and on local taxes such as VAT. So the right price for you is the one the app shows before the free month starts.

I can see that many of you already have 200, 300, even 400 sales in FlipperHelper, so it looks like business is going well, and I am glad. I will keep building FlipperHelper for my wife, whose business is growing too, and for you. If it does what you need, you will keep Pro for years, and next to years one free month is nothing. I want a deal that is good for both of us.

What comes next

Not everyone sells on eBay. Tell me which other platforms you want connected in the same way. If I can, I will add them to the same subscription. I do not plan to charge for eBay and then again for each next platform. I want one subscription that covers it all.

I am also thinking about bank sync. You connect your bank, and its payments either show up as expenses by themselves, or wait in a list where you choose what to add. FlipperHelper could also see whether a payment was fuel or a McDonald's, and record its currency and exchange rate. If you want this, email me, and please say which bank you use and on which continent you live. Banks work very differently from country to country, and FlipperHelper users live all over the world, so I want to find something that works for most of you.

Quick answers

Is FlipperHelper still free?

Yes. The app stays free with no ads, and no free feature goes away. Pro is an optional subscription.

What does FlipperHelper Pro add?

Sync between your devices, a team of two people, the eBay connection, and the web portal with the history of changes and CSV export.

How many people can be in a team?

Two: you and one member. One account can be in up to 5 teams, its own team included.

Does my team member need their own Pro?

No. The owner's Pro covers the team.

How far back does the eBay connection look?

90 days, for listings that ended and for orders, if you added an item to FlipperHelper in the 30 days before you connect.

Is there a free trial of FlipperHelper Pro?

Yes. The first month is free on the monthly and the yearly plan, on iPhone and on Android.
