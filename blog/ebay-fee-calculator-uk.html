Why this matters: fees catch people off guard

A post on r/eBaySellerAdvice is titled “Learned a hard lesson today.” The seller listed a book for $4. They didn’t know that eBay includes the shipping cost in its fees. They said the fees took 50% of the sale price. After that, they couldn’t afford a box to ship the book in.

In another post, someone bought a golf bag at Goodwill (a US charity shop) for $15 and sold it on eBay for $68. The buyer paid $111 for shipping from Las Vegas to New York. The seller paid a $26 transaction fee and a $20 promoted listing fee. The seller said the profit was only $6.

These are US examples in US dollars. UK-based private sellers now pay no final value fee (see below). But eBay UK charges business sellers fees on the total sale, including postage. If you don’t calculate your fees and postage before you list, your “profit” can disappear. Use the calculator above to check this before you list.

How eBay fees work

Since 1 October 2024, UK-based private sellers pay no final value fees on eBay (source: eBay UK’s selling FAQs). They get 300 free listings per month. If you exceed 300 listings, you pay 35p per additional listing. If the delivery address is outside the UK, private sellers pay a 3% international fee, unless they use eBay International Shipping.

eBay says a private account is the best option if you sell casually, for example items you no longer want. eBay’s business seller policy says you must register as a business if, for example, you sell items you bought to resell. HMRC has its own rules. For more on this, see our HMRC trading allowance guide.

Business sellers pay these fees on every sale (per eBay’s fee schedule for business sellers). The final value fee has two parts: a percentage and a per-order fee.

  • Final value fee percentage: A percentage of the total sale (item + postage). It is 9.9–12.9% in most categories (11.9% for clothes). It is lower for phones and consoles and up to 14.9% for jewellery
  • Per-order fee: 30p on orders of £10 or less, 40p on orders over £10 (40p since 12 February 2026). In some categories, for example Home, Furniture & DIY and Collectables, it is 10p on orders of £10 or less. The 10p fee is for business sellers with a UK registered address
  • Regulatory operating fee: 0.35% of the total sale

eBay shows these fees excluding VAT. eBay adds VAT to its fees where VAT applies. The calculator above adds VAT at 20%, the UK standard rate, to all of these fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much does eBay take from a sale UK?

UK-based private sellers pay no final value fee and no regulatory operating fee. Business sellers pay about 16–17% of the total sale on a £20–30 clothing sale. This total includes the final value fee, the per-order fee, the regulatory fee and 20% VAT on the fees.

How do I calculate eBay fees?

Total fee = (sale price + postage) × category fee % + per-order fee + (sale price + postage) × 0.35%. Then add 20% VAT on all fees. Or just use the calculator above.
