Get FlipperHelper on Android

Early access (closed testing) on Google Play

The Android app is currently in early access on Google Play, so getting it takes two quick steps. It’s free — no ads, no account required. Use the same Google account for both steps.

Step 1 — Join the testers group (required)

You must be a member of the testers group for the install link to work — Google only lets group members into the early access build.

Join the testers group

Tap Join group and you’re in immediately.

Step 2 — Opt in and install

Once you’ve joined, open the testing link below on the same Google account, tap Become a tester, then install from Google Play.

Opt in & install on Google Play

If the app isn’t visible right after opting in, give it a few minutes — sometimes up to an hour.

Good to know

  • These are pre-release builds, so expect the occasional rough edge — that’s exactly what we want to hear about.
  • Please use the app over the next couple of weeks and send feedback — it genuinely helps us launch.
  • Feedback: reply in the group, use the in-app feedback option in Settings, or email andreevichprudnikov@gmail.com.
  • Want to leave later? Opt out at the same testing link, or leave the group.

On an iPhone instead? Download FlipperHelper on the App Store — it’s already live there.
